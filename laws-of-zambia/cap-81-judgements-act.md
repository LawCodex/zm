## CHAPTER 81 — 10of 1961

JUDGMENTS

An Act to provide for the payment of interest on judgment debts. [5th May, 1961]

1.

This Act may be cited as the Judgments Act.

2\. Every judgment, order, or decree of the High Court or of a subordinate court whereby any sum of money, or any costs, charges or expenses, is or are to be payable to any person shall carry interest at the rate of six per centum per annum from the time of entering up such judgment, order, or decree until the same shall be satisfied, and such interest may be levied under a writ of execution on such judgment, order, or decree.
