SUPREME COURT AND HIGH COURT (NUMBER OF JUDGES)

An Act to prescribe the number of Supreme Court judges and of puisne judges of the High Court. [29th July, 1988]

### Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Supreme Court and High Court (Number of Judges) Act.

### Section 2 — Number of Supreme Court judges

There shall be nine judges of the Supreme Court, including the Chief Justice and the Deputy Chief Justice.

### Section 3 — Number of puisne judges

There shall be thirty puisne judges of the High Court. (As amended by Act No. 20 of 1977, No. 21 of 1988 and No. 10 of 1997)
