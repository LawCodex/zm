CHAPTER 191

15 of 1961 Government Notice 497 of 1964

BILLS OF SALE (REGISTRATION)

An Act to remove certain doubts relating to the registration of Bills of Sale and for matters connected therewith. [11th August, 1961]

### Section 1 — Short title

This Act may be cited as the Bills of Sale (Registration) Act, and shall be read as one with the Lands and Deeds Registry Act.

Short title Cap. 185

### Section 2 — Non-application of certain provisions of the Bills of Sale Acts

It is hereby declared for the removal of doubt that the provisions of the Bills of Sale Acts, 1878-1882, of the United Kingdom, requiring the registration and re-registration of Bills of Sale under those Acts, are not in force and have not, since the commencement of the Lands and Deeds Registry Act, been in force in Zambia.
