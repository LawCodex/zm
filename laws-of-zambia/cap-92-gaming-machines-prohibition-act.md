# Gaming Machines (Prohibition) Act

REPUBLIC OF ZAMBIA

THE GAMING MACHINES (PROHIBITION) ACT

CHAPTER 92 OF THE LAWS OF ZAMBIA CHAPTER 92 THE GAMING MACHINES (PROHIBITION) ACT THE GAMING MACHINES (PROHIBITION) ACT

Act No. 2 of 1974 13 of 1994

An Act to repeal the Gaming Machines Act and to prohibit the keeping of gaming machines. [22nd February 1974]

### Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Gaming Machines (Prohibition) Act.

Short title

### Section 2

In this Act, unless context otherwise requires-

Interpretation

"gaming machine" means a machine for playing a game of chance, being a game which requires no action by any player other than the actuation or manipulation of the machine.

### Section 3

(1) Any person who, not being the holder of a casino licence under the Casino Act-

(a)

keeps; or

(b)

allows to be kept;

on any premises a gaming machine for the purpose of gaming shall be guilty of an offence and liable to a fine not exceeding ten thousand penalty units or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding one year, or to both.

Penalty for keeping gaming machine for the purpose of gaming. Cap 157

(2) When any person is convicted of an offence under the provisions of sub-section

(1), the court by which he is convicted shall, in addition to any fine or imprisonment imposed under subsection (1), order the forfeiture to the Government of the gaming machine which was the subject-matter of the offence. (As amended by Act No. 13 of 1994)

### Section 4

The Gaming Machine Act is hereby repealed.

Repeal of Cap. 431 of the 1971 edition
