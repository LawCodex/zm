# The Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (Amendment) Act, 2021

Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (Amendment)

[No. 30 of 2021 253

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 30 of 2021 Date of Assent: 19th May, 2021 An Act to amend the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport Act, 2014. [20th May, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport Act, 2014, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “ Higher Education Authority” means the Higher Education Authority established under the Higher Education Act, 2013;; and “Zambia Qualifications Authority” means the Zambia Qualifications Authority established under the Zambia Qualifications Authority Act, 2011.

## Section 3

Section 7 (2) of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of paragraph (d); and

(b) renumbering of paragraphs (e),(f), (g), (h), (i) and (j) as paragraphs (d), (e),(f),(g),(h) and (i), respectively.

## Section 4

Section 24 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (b) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(b) has attended a university, college or school registered by the Higher Education Authority and holds a degree or other qualification for this class of membership accredited and recognised by the Zambia Qualifications Authority;.
