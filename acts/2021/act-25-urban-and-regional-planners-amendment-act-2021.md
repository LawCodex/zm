# Urban and Regional Planners (Amendment) Act, 2021

Urban and Regional Planners (Amendment)

[No. 25 of 2021 243

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 25 of 2021 Date of Assent: 19th May, 2021 An Act to amend the Urban and Regional Planners Act, 2011. [20th May, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Urban and Regional Planners (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Urban and Regional Planners Act, 2011, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order:

Amendment of section 2

Act No. 4 of 2011

“Higher Education Authority” means the Higher Education Authority established under the Higher Education Act, 2013;; and

Act No. 4 of 2013

“Zambia Qualifications Authority” means the Zambia Qualifications Authority established under the Zambia Qualifications Authority Act, 2011.

## Section 3

Section 15 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (b) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 15

(b) holds a qualification accredited and recognised by the Zambia Qualifications Authority;.

## Section 4

Section 29(b) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “and training” immediately after the word “development”.

Amendment of section 29
