# The Money Lenders (Amendment) Act, 2021

Money-Lenders (Amendment)

[No. 51 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 51 of 2021

Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 ENTITLED

An Act to amend the Money-Lenders Act. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Money-Lenders (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the MoneyLenders Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2022.

## Section 2

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “four hundred and fifty fee units or, if the licence be taken out not more than six months before the expiration thereof, three hundred fee units” and the substitution therefor of the words “thirty-three thousand, three hundred and thirty-three or, if the licence be taken out not more than six months before the expiration thereof, twenty-two thousand, two hundred and twenty-two fee units”.

Enactment Short title and commencement Cap. 398 Amendment of section 3
