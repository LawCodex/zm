ACT No. 50 of 2021 Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 An Act to amend the Betting Control Act. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Betting Control (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Betting Control Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap.166

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2022.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place in alphabetical order:

Amendment of section 2

“small business enterprise” means a business enterprise whose total investment, excluding land and buildings, and annual turnover and the number of persons employed by the enterprise does not exceed the numerical value or number prescribed;

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 12.

## Section 4

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 14 and the substitution therefor of the following: 14.

## Section 5

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new section immediately after section 28:

Savings and transitional provisions

29\. (1) A licence issued to a bookmaker prior to 1st January, 2022 shall be valid for a period of six months, or until its expiry, revocation or surrender in accordance with its terms, whichever is the earlier.

(2) A bookmaker whose licence expires or is due to expire under subsection (1) may apply for the renewal of the licence in accordance with this Act.
