# The Accountants (Amendment) Act, 2021

Accountants (Amendment)

[No. 24 of 2021 241

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 24 of 2021 Date of Assent: 19th May, 2021

An Act to amend the Accountants Act, 2008. [20th May, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Accountants (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Accountants Act, 2008, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (2), by the—

(i) deletion of paragraph (e); and

(ii) renumbering of paragraphs (f), (g), (h), (i), (j), (k),

(l), (m), and (n) as paragraphs (e), (f), (g), (h),

(i), (j), (k), (l) and(m), respectively; and

Act No. 13 of 2008 Amendment of section 5

(c) in subsection (3), by the —

(i) deletion of paragraph (c); and

(ii) renumbering of paragraph (d) as paragraph (c).

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 13.

Repeal of section 13
