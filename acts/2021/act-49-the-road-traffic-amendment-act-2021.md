# The Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2021

Road Traffic (Amendment)

[No. 49 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 49 of 2021

Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 An Act to amend the Road Traffic Act, 2002. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Road Traffic (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Road Traffic Act, 2002, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2)

Enactment Short title and commencement Act No. 11 of 2002

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2022.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of the Second Schedule and the substitution therefor of the Second Schedule set out in the Appendix.

Repeal and replacement of Second Schedule

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K4.00 each.
