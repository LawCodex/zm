# The Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2021

Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) [No. 15 of 2021 197

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 15 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Zambia Revenue Authority Act. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Revenue Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 321

## Section 2

Section 10 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraphs (a) and (b) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(a) a representative of the Secretary to the Treasury; and

Amendment of section 10

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General.
