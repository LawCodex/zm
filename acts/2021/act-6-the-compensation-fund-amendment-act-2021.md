# The Compensation Fund (Amendment) Act, 2021

Compensation Fund (Amendment)

[No. 6 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Compensation Fund Act, 2016. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Compensation Fund (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the 5 Compensation Fund Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the Principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “ Permanent Secretary”

## Section 3

Section 4 (2) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (a) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(a) the Chairperson;.

Enactment Short title Act No. 43 of 2016 Amendment of section 2 Amendment of section 4
