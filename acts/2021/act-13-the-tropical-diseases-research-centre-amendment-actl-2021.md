# The Tropical Diseases Research Centre (Amendment) Actl, 2021

Tropical Diseases Research Centre (Amendment)

[No. 13 of 2021 193

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 13 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Tropical Diseases Research Centre Act. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Tropical Diseases Research Centre (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Tropical Diseases Research Centre Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 5(1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (b) and the substitution therefor of the following:
