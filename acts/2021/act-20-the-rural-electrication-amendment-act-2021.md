# The Rural Electrication (Amendment) Act, 2021

Rural Electrification (Amendment)

[No. 20 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 20 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Rural Electrification Act, 2003. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Rural Electrification (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Rural Electrification Act, 2003, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Act No. 20 of 2003

## Section 2

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new subsections immediately after subsection 2:

Amendment of section 4

(3) The Minister may give the Authority general or specific directions relating to the performance of its functions and the Authority shall give effect to these directions to the extent that the directions are not inconsistent with this Act.

(4) The Minister may enter into performance contracts with theAuthority for a specified period which shall be consistent with the provisions of this Act.

## Section 3

Section 6(1) of the principal Act is amended by the —

(a) deletion of paragraphs (a), (b) and (c) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 6
