# The Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2021) Act, 2021

[No. 42 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 42 of 2021 Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 An Act to approve excess expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies aggregating nineteen billion, three hundred forty seven million, nine hundred eighty one thousand, eight hundred sixty seven kwacha. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Short title Appropriation (2021) Act, 2021.

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there Excess may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not expenditure appropriation exceeding in the aggregate nineteen billion, three hundred forty seven million, nine hundred eighty one thousand, eight hundred sixty seven kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2021.
