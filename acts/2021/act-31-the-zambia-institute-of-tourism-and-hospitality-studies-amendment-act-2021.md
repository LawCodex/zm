# The Zambia Institute of Tourism and Hospitality Studies (Amendment) Act, 2021

Zambia Institute for Tourism and Hospitality Studies (Amendment) [No. 31 of 2021 255

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 31 of 2021 Date of Assent: 19th May, 2021

An Act to amend the Zambia Institute for Tourism and Hospitality Studies Act, 2016. [ 20th May, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Institute for Tourism and Hospitality Studies (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Institute for Tourism and Hospitality Studies Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place in alphabetical order: “Higher Education Authority” means the Higher Education Authority established under the Higher Education Act, 2013;.

## Section 3

Section 5 (1) is amended by the deletion of paragraph (a) and the substitution therefore of the following:

(a) Conduct training programmes accredited by the Higher Education Authority of persons in the tourism and hospitality sectors.
