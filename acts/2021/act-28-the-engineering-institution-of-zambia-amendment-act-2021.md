# The Engineering Institution of Zambia (Amendment) Act, 2021

Engineering Institution of Zambia (Amendment) [No. 28 of 2021 249

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 28 of 2021 Date of Assent: 19th May, 2021 AnAct to amend the Engineering Institution of Zambia Act, 2010. [20thMay, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Engineering Institution of Zambia (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Engineering Institution of Zambia Act, 2010, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Enactment Short title

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order:

Amendment of section 2

Act No. 17 of 2010

“Higher Education Authority” means the Higher Education Authority established under the Higher Education Act, 2013; and

Act No. 4 of 2013

“Zambia Qualifications Authority” means the Zambia Qualifications Authority established under the Zambia Qualifications Authority Act, 2011;.

## Section 6

Section 62 (2) of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (f), by the insertion of the words “accredited and recognised by the Zambia Qualifications Authority” immediately after the word “qualifications”;

(b) by the deletion of paragraph (g); and

(c) by the renumbering of paragraphs (h), (i),(j),(k) and (l) as paragraphs (g),(h), (i),(j) and (k), respectively.
