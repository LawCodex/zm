# The Zambia Institute of Diplomacy and International Studies (Amendment) Act, 2021

Zambia Institute of Diplomacy and International Studies (Amendment)

[No. 14 of 2021 195

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 14 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Zambia Institute of Diplomacy and International Studies Act. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Institute of Diplomacy and International Studies (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Institute of Diplomacy and International Studies Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of—

(a) the figure “(1)” between the figure “6”and the word “Subject”; and

(b) the following new subsections:

(2) The Minister may give the Board general or specific directions relating to the performance of its functions and the Board shall give effect to these directions to the extent that the directions are not inconsistent with this Act.

(3) The Minister may enter into performance contracts with the Board for a specified period which shall be consistent with the provisions of this Act.
