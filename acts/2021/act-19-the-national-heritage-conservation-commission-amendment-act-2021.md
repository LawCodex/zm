# The National Heritage Conservation Commission (Amendment) Act, 2021

National Heritage Conservation Commission (Amendment)

[No. 19 of 2021 205

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 19 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the National Heritage Conservation Commission Act. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the National Heritage Conservation Commission (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the National Heritage Conservation Commission Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 5(1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (b) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(b) a representative of the ministry responsible for heritage;.

Enactment Short title Cap. 173 Amendment of section 5
