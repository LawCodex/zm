# The Service Commissions (Amendment) Act, 2021

Service Commisions (Amendment)

[No. 12 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 12 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Service Commissions Act, 2016 [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Service Commissions (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Service Commissions Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Act No. 10 of 2016

## Section 2

Section 5(1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (d) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 5

(d)

a representative of the Public Service Management

Division from Cabinet Office;.
