# The Supplementary Appropriation (2021) Act, 2021

[No. 52 of 2021 701

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT

No. 52 of 2021 Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2021, not exceeding in the aggregate nineteen billion, six hundred thirty two million, eight hundred sixtytwo thousand, one hundred thirty-four kwacha. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2021) Act, 2021.

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not exceeding in the aggregate nineteen billion, six hundred thirty-two million, eight hundred sixtytwo thousand, one hundred thirty-four kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2021.

Enactment Short title Supplementary appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P. O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K8.00 each
