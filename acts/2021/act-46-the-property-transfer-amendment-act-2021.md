# The Property Transfer (Amendment) Act, 2021

Property Transfer Tax (Amendment) [No. 46 of 2021

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 46 of 2021

Date of Assent: 29th December, 2021 An Act to amend the Property Transfer Tax Act. [30th December, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Property Transfer Tax (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Property Transfer Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 340

(2)

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2022.

(a)

(a) a land in the Republic;
