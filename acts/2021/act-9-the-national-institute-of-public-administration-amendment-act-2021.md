# The National Institute of Public Administration (Amendment) Act, 2021

National Institute of Public Administration [No. 9 of 2021 (Amendment)

185

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 9 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the National Institute of Public Administration Act, 1998. [24th March, 2021 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the National Institute of Public Administration (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the National Institute of Public Administration Act, 1998, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 5(1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (a) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 5

(a) a representative of the Public Service Management Division, Cabinet Office;.

## Section 3

Section 23(3) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (a) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Act No. 15 of 1998

Amendment of section 23

(a) a representative of the ministry responsible for labour and social security;.
