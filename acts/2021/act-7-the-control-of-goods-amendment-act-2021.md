# The Control of Goods (Amendment) Act, 2021

Control of Goods (Amendment)

[No. 7 of 2021 181

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 7 of 2021 Date of Assent: 23rd March, 2021 An Act to amend the Control of Goods Act. [24th March, 2021 Enactment ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Control of Goods Short title (Amendment) Act, 2021, and shall be read as one with the Cap. 421 Control of Goods Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 4G of the principal Act is amended by— Amendment of

(a) the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor section 4G of the following: (1.) (a) the Director responsible for foreign trade in the ministry responsible for commerce, trade and industry;

(b) the Director responsible for domestic trade in the ministry responsible for commerce, trade and industry;

(c) the Director responsible for industry in the ministry responsible for commere, trade and industry;

(d) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i) budget and economic affairs;
