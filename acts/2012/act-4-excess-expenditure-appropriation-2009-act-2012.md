# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2009) Act 2012

Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2009)

[No. 4 of 2012 61

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 4 of 2012 Date of Assent:12th April, 2012 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of moneys aggregating one hundred and forty-one billion, one hundred and ninety-nine million, five hundred and sixtynine thousand, one hundred and eighty seven kwacha. [16th April, 2012 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2009) Act, 2012.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2009, of moneys indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2009, and the Supplementary

Excess expenditure approved

Appropriation (2009) Act, 2011, is hereby approved.
