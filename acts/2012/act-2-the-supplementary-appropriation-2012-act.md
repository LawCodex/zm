An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating four billion, four hundred and sixty-seven million, five thousand, five hundred and four kwacha, required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2011. [31st July, 2013

P.t

ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia. This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2011) Act, 2013.

Enactment

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic, such moneys, indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Appropriation Act, 2011.
