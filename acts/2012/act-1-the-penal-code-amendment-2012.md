# The Penal Code (Amendment) (2012)

Penal Code (Amendment)

[No. 1 of 2012 1

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 1 of 2012 Date of Assent:12th April, 2012 An Act to amend the Penal Code. [ 16th April, 2012 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Penal Code (Amendment) Act, Short title 2012, and shall be read as one with the Penal Code Act, in this Act Cap. 87 referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section ninetynine and the substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement of section 99

99\. A prosecution for an offence under sections ninetyseven or ninety-eight shall not be instituted except by, or with, the sanction of the Director of Public Prosecutions.

Consent of Director of Public Prosecutions
