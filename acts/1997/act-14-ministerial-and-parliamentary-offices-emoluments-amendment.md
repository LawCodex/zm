# Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) (Amendment)

Date of Assent: 12th April, 1997 An Act to amend the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) Act, 1992 [18th April, 1997 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) (Amendment) Act, 1997, and shall be read as one with the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) Act, 1992, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section four of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) Subject to the other provisions of this section, there shall be paid to the holder of an office specified in the First Schedule, as from 1st November, 1991, agraluity amounting to thirty-five per centum of the last earned gross annual emolument, multiplied by the number of years served on a pro-rata basis'. Provided that gratuity paid to any holder of such office under the principal Act before the commencement of this Act shall be deducted from such gratuity.
