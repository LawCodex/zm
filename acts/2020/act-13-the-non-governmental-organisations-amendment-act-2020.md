# The Non-Governmental Organisations (Amendment) Act, 2020

Non-Governmental Organisations (Amendment) [No. 13 of 2020

255

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 13 of 2020 Date of Assent: 30th November, 2020 An Act to amend the Non-Governmental Organisations Act, 2009. [2nd December, 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Non-Governmental Organisations (Amendment) Act, 2020, and shall be read as one with the Non-Governmental Organisations Act, 2009, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 4 of the Principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition:

Amendment of section 4

Act No. 16 of 2009

“Financial Intelligence Centre” means the Financial Intelligence Centre established under the Financial Act No. 46 of 2010 Intelligence Centre Act, 2010; “proliferation financing” has the meaning assigned to the words of the Anti-Terrorism and Non-Proliferation Act, 2018; Act No. 6 of 2018

“serious offence” has the meaning assigned to the words in the Forfeiture of Proceeds of Crime Act, 2010; and Act No. 19 of 2010

“terrorism financing” has the meaning assigned to the words Act No. 6 of in the Anti-Terrorism and Non-Proliferation Act, 2018.
