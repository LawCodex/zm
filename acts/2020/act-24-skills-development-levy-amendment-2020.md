# Skills Development Levy (Amendment), 2020

Skills Development Levy (Amendment)

[No. of 24 2020 349

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 24 of 2020 Date of Assent: 18th December, 2020 An Act to amend the Skills Development Levy Act, 2016.

[18th December, 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Skills Development Levy (Amendment) Act, 2020, and shall be read as one with the Skills Development Levy Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Act No. 46 of 2016

## Section 2

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (3) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 4

(3) A levy payable by an employer shall not be deducted from the emoluments of an employee.
