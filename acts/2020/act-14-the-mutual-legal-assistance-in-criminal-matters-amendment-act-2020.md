# The Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters (Amendment) Act, 2020

Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal [No. 14 of 2020 257 Matters (Amendment)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 14 of 2020 Date of Assent: 30th November, 2020 An Act to amend the Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters Act. [ 2nd December, 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters (Amendment) Act, 2020, and shall be read as one with the Mutual Legal Assistance in Criminal Matters Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 10 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection 4:

Amendment of section 10

Cap. 98

(5) The Minister may, by statutory instrument, prescribe measures for monitoring the execution of a request made under this section.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K4.00 each.
