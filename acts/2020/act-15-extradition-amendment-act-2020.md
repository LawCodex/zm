# Extradition (Amendment) Act, 2020

Extradition (Amendment) 2020

[ No. 15 of 2020 259

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 15 of 2020 Date of Assent: 3rd December, 2020 An Act to amend the Extradition Act. [8th December, 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Extradition (Amendment) Act, 2020, and shall be read as one with the Extradition Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the —

Amendment of section 6

(a)

Cap. 94

figure “(1)” immediately after “6”; and

(b) following new subsections:

(2)

The Attorney-General may apply simplified extradition measures where the person sought by a requesting State consents before a Magistrate to be surrendered to that State.

(3) The Attorney-General may, by statutory instrument, prescribe measures for simplified extradition and for monitoring the execution of a request made under this section.
