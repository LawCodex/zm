# The Landlord and Tenant (Business Premises) (Amendment Act), 2020

Landlord and Tenant (Business Premises) [No. 5 of 2020 93 Amendment

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 5 of 2020 Date of Assent: 23rd October, 2020 An Act to amend the Landlord and Tenant (Business Premises) Act. [ 26th October, 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Landlord and Tenant (Business Premises) (Amendment) Act, 2020 and shall be read as one with the Landlord and Tenant (Business Premises) Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “court” and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of Section 2

Cap. 193

“court”means—

(a) in relation to premises for which the rent demanded exceeds one hundred thousand kwacha per year, the High Court; or

(b) in relation to all other premises, a subordinate court of the first class.
