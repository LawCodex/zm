# The Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2020) Act, 2020

[No. 9 of 2020

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 9 of 2020 Date of Assent: 23rd November 2020 An Act to approve excess expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies aggregating fifteen billion, four hundred sixty-four million, two hundred seventeen thousand, four hundred seventy-four kwacha. [23rd November 2020 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Short title Appropriation (2020) Act, 2020.

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there Excess may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not expenditure appropriation exceeding in the aggregate fifteen billion, four hundred sixty-four million, two hundred seventeen thousand, four hundred seventyfour kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the First and Second Schedules, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2020.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K12,000
