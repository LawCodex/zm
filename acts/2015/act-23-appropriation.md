# Appropriation

[No. 23 of 2015

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 23 of 2015 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2015 An Act to authorise expenditure from the general revenues of the Republic of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2016, not exceeding in the aggregate fiftythree billion, one hundred and thirtyfive million, eight hundred and twenty-five thousand, three hundred and sixty four kwacha. [24th December, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1 — Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 2015.

Short title

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the general revenues of the Republic, such monies, not exceeding in the aggregate fifty-three billion, one hundred and thirty-five million, eight hundred and twenty-five thousand, three hundred and sixty four kwacha as may be required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2016.

Appropriation

(2) The monies referred to in subsection (1) shall be appropriated to the services of the Republic specified in the second column of the First Schedule and the Second Schedule respectively, in amounts not exceeding the amounts indicated in the third column of the First Schedule and Second Schedule respectively.

(3) On the written authority of the Secretary to the Treasury, the amounts shown under the sub-heads in the First Schedule and under the programmes in the Second Schedule, may be varied, except that the amounts appropriated to each sub-head in the First Schedule and the amounts appropriated to each programme in the Second Schedule shall not be thereby altered. Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K22.00
