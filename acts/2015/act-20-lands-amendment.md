# Lands (Amendment)

Lands (Amendment)

[No. 20 of 2015

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 20 of 2015 Date of Assent: 17th December, 2015 An Act to amend the Lands Act [ 24th December, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Lands (Amendment) Act, 2015, and shall be read as one with the Lands Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 184

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2016.

## Section 2

Section four of the principal Act is amended—

(a) by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 4

(1) The President shall not alienate land under subsection (2) or (3) of section three without receiving consideration, in money, for such alienation and ground rent for such land except where the alienation is for a public purpose.; and

(b) in subsection (2)—

(i) by the insertion in paragraph (g), immediately after the word “purposes”, of the words “by the Government”; and

(ii) by the insertion in paragraph (h), immediately after the word “resources”, of the words “by the Government”. Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K2.00 each.

No. 20 of 2015]

Amendment of section 16

Lands (Amendment)

## Section 3

Section sixteen of the principal Act is amended in paragraph

(b) of subsection (2) by the deletion of the words “seventy-five” and the substitution therefor of the words “twenty-five”.
