# The Gold (Repeal)

Gold Trade (Repeal)

[No. 12 of 2015

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 12 of 2015 Date of Assent: 14th August, 2015 An Act to repeal the Gold Trade Act [14th August, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Gold Trade (Repeal) Act, 2015.

## Section 2

## Section 3

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires— “ Director ” means the Director of Mines appointed under the Mines and Minerals Development Act, 2015; and “ repealed Act ” means the Gold Trade Act.

(1) The Gold Trade Act, 1912, is hereby repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a person who, at the commencement of this Act, is a holder of a licence or permit issued under the repealed Act shall, within six months after such commencement, apply to the Director for a licence, except that the Director shall only grant a permit for the unexpired period for which it was granted under the repealed Act.
