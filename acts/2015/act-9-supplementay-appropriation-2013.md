# Supplementay Appropriation (2013)

Supplementary Appropriation (2013)

[No. 9 of 2015 153

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 9 of 2015 Date of Assent: 14th August, 2015 An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of monies aggregating four billion, one hundred and seventy-two million, five hundred and forty-eight thousand, five hundred and sixty-eight kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2013. [14th August, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (2013) Act, 2015.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is confirmed that there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such monies indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Appropriation Act, 2012.

Supplementary appropriation
