An Act to amend the Value Added Tax Act. [24th December, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2015, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2016, except for sections five and six, which shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may appoint by statutory instrument.

## Section 2

Section two of the principal Act is amended by the insertion, in the appropriate place, of the following: “fiscal cash register” means an electronic device comprising, among others, electronic cash registers, fiscal printers and electronic cash register, fiscal printers and electronic signature devices that are approved by the CommissionerGeneral with fiscal memory and capacity to generate or produce tax invoices and other reports as may be required by the Commissioner-General;.
