# Referendum

Referendum (Amendment)

[No. 5 of 2015

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 5 of 2015 Date of Assent: 14th August, 2015 An Act to amend the Referendum Act [14th August, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Referendum (Amendment) Act, 2015, and shall be read as one with the Referendum Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 14

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new section:

Insertion of new section 1A

1A.

In this Act unless the context otherwise requires— “ Commission ” means the Electoral Commission established by the Constitution; “Director” means the Director appointed under the Electoral Commission Act; and

Interepretation Cap. 1 Cap. 17

“referendum officer” means a person appointed by the Commission to be—

(a) a district referendum officer;

(b) a returning officer;

(c) a presiding officer;

(d) a polling assistant; or

(e) a counting assistant; and includes a person appointed by a referendum officer under subsection (2) of section eight or the Director where functions are conferred on the Director under this Act, in respect of a refereundum.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K2.00 each.

No. 5 of 2015]

Amendment of section 3

Repeal and replacement of section 4

## Section 3

Repeal and replacement of section 6 Procedure of Commission Cap. 17

Section three of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (1), by the deletion of the word “All” and the substitution therefor of the words “Subject to subsection (2), all”;

(b) by the insertion, immediately after subsection (1), of the following new subsection:

(2) Any question put to a referendum under Article 79 of the Constitution shall be voted on by not less than fifty percent of persons entitled to be registered as voters for the purposes of Presidential and Parliamentary elections.; and

(c) by the consequential renumbering of subsections (2) and

(3) as (3) and (4), respectively.

## Section 4

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section four and the substitution therefor of the following: 4.

## Section 5

The Commission shall be responsible for the administration of this Act and for supervising a referendum held pursuant to section two.

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section five.

## Section 6

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section six and the substitution therefor of the following: 6.

Section eight of the Electoral Commision Act applies to the Commission.

## Section 7

Section eight of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (6).
