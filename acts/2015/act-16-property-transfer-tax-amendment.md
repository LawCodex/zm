Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Property Transfer Tax (Amendment) Act, 2015, and shall be read as one with the Property Transfer Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2)

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2016.
