# The Excess Expenditure Appropriation

Excess Expenditure Appropriation

[No. 48 of 2016

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 48 of 2016 Date of Assent: 27th December, 2016 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of monies aggregating seven billion, four hundred and three million, seven hundred thousand kwacha. [27th December, 2016 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation Act, 2016.

Short Title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2016, of the monies indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2015, Supplementary Appropriation Act, 2016 and Supplementary Appropriation (2016) Act, 2016, is approved.
