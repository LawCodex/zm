# The Local Government (Amendment)

Local Government (Amendment) [No. 17 of 2016

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 17 of 2016 Date of Assent: 6th June, 2016 An Act to amend the Local Government Act. [7th June, 2016 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Local Government (Amendment) Act, 2016, and shall be read as one with the Local Government Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 281

## Section 2

Section two of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition “Commission”.

Amendment of section 2

## Section 3

Section forty-five A of the principal Act is amended in subsection (2) by the insertion, immediately after the word “Councils”, of the words “to pay the salaries of principal officers and officers of a Council and”.

Amendment of section 45A
