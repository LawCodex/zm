# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2013)

Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2013)

[No. 37 of 2016

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 37 of 2016 Date of Assent: 18th November, 2016 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of monies aggregating five hundred and seventy-three thousand, six hundred and thirty-five kwacha. [ 22nd November, 2016 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2013) Act, 2016.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year which ended on 31st December, 2013, of the monies indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2013, and the Supplementary Appropriation (2013) Act, 2015, is approved.
