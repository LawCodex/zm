# The Supplementary Appropriation (2016)

[No. 39 of 2016

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 39 of 2016 Date of Assent: 25th November, 2016 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the general revenues of the Republic of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December 2016, not exceeding in the aggregate three billion, nine hundred and forty-nine million, six hundred and forty-four thousand and eight hundred and fifteen kwacha. [28th November, 2016 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Short title

(2016) Act, 2016.

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, Supplementary there may be expended from the general revenues of the Republic Appropriation such monies indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the First and Second Schedules being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2016.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K12.00
