# Appropriation

[No. 5 of 2005 17

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 5 of 2005 Date of Assent: 1 st April, 2005 An Act to authorise expenditure from the general revenues of the Republic of moneys required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on the 31st December, 2005, not exceeding in the aggregate nine trillion, seven hundred and seventy-nine billion and twenty five million, three hundred and seventy thousand, four hundred and thirteen kwacha. [1st April, 2005 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 2005.

Short title

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the general revenues of the Republic such monies, not exceeding in the aggregate nine trillion, seven hundred and seventy-nine billion and twenty five million, three hundred and seventy thousand, four hundred and thirteen kwacha, as may be required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on the 31st December, 2005.

Application

(2) The monies referred to in subsection (1) shall be appropriated to the services of the Republic specified in the second column of the Schedule in amounts not exceeding the amounts indicated in the Third Column of the Schedule.

(3) On the written authority of the Secretary to the Treasury, Ministry of Finance and National Planning, the amounts shown under the sub-heads of estimates may be varied: Provided that—

(a) the amount appropriated to each Head shall not thereby be altered; and

(b) the amount shown under Head 20 or 21 shall not be varied.
