# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2002)

Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2002) 2005

[No. 7 of 2005 37

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 7 of 2005 Date of Assent: 25th April, 2005 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of the moneys aggregating forty-five billion four hundred and eighty-one million one hundred and eighty-seven thousand seven hundred and sixty five Kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended 31st December, 2002. [17th May, 2005 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2002) Act, 2005.

Short Title

## Section 2 — The expenditure on the services of the Republic during Supplimentary

Appropriations the financial year ended on the 31 st December, 2002, of the moneys indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the money appropriated for services by the Appropriation Act, 2002 and Supplementary Act No. 6 of 2002 Appropriation (2002) Act, 2004, is hereby approved.
