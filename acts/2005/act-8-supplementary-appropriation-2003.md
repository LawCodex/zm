# Supplementary Appropriation (2003)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 8 of 2005 Date ofAssent: 25 April, 2005 An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating three hundred fifty-one billion, one hundred forty-one million, one hundred thirty-one thousand, one hundred and thirty-nine kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended 31st December, 2003. [ 17th May, 2005 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2003) Act, 2005.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such moneys, in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Appropriation Act, 2003.
