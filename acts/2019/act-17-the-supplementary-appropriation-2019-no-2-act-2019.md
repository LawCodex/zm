# The Supplementary Appropriation (2019) (No. 2) Act, 2019

Supplementary Appropriation (2019) (No. 2) [No. 17 of 2019

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 17 of 2019 Date of Assent: 27th December, 2019 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2019, not exceeding in aggregate one billion, one hundred and seventy-five million, seven hundred and sixty-two thousand, seven hundred and fifty-six kwacha. [27th December, 2019 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation (2019 (No. 2) Act, 2019.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not exceeding in aggregate one billion, one hundred and seventy-five million, seven hundred and sixty-two thousand, seven hundred and fifty-six kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the First Schedule and Second Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2019.

Supplementary appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K8.00 each.
