No. 53 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Zambia Medicines and Medical Supplies Agency Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia Medicines and Medical Supplies Agency (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Medicines and Medical Supplies Agency Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsections (1) and (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Agency which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i) health; and

(ii) finance;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Health Professions Council of Zambia;

(d) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Purchasing and Supply;
