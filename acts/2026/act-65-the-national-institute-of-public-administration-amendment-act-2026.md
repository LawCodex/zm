# The National Institute of Public Administration ( Amendment) Act, 2026

[No. 65 of 2026 749

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 65 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June,2026 An act to amend the National Institute of Public Administration Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Institute of Public Administration (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Institute of Public Administration Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 152

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Council holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Council in accordance with this Act.

## Section 3

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is constituted the governing Council of the Institute which consists of the following parttime members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative of the ministry responsible for labour;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Public Service Management Division, Cabinet Office;

(d) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants;

(e) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Human Resource Management;

(f)a representative of the Zambia Institute of Management;

(g) two representatives of federations of trade unions; and

(h) one person from the private sector with knowledge and experience in business and commercial regulation.;

(b) insertion of the following new subsections immediately after subsection (2):

(3) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

(4) A person shall not be appointed as a member of the Council if that person is—

(a) an undischarged bankrupt;

(b) legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member; or

(c) convicted of an offence under this Act or any other law and sentenced to a term of imprisonment exceeding six months without an option of a fine.; and

(c) renumbering of subsection (3) as subsection (5).
