# The Public Roads (Amendment) Act , 2026

Public Roads (Amendment)

[No. 19 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT

No. 19 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Public Roads Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Public Roads (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Public Roads Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 472

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Engineering Institution of Zambia” means the Engineering Institution of Zambia established under the Engineering Institution of Zambia Act; “legally disqualified” means the absence of legal capacity as provided under section 4 of the Mental Health Act; “Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport” means the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport established under the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport Act; and

Public Roads (Amendment)

“Zambia Institute of Quantity Surveyors” means the Zambia Institute of Quantity Surveyors established under the Zambia Institute of Quantity Surveyors Act, 2024.

## Section 3

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsections (1), (2) and (3) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Agency which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a)

a representative each from the ministries responsible for—

(i) infrastructure;

(ii) finance; and

(iii) local government;

(b)

a representative of the AttorneyGeneral;

(c)

a representative of the Engineering Institution of Zambia;

(d)

a representative of the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport;

(e) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Quantity Surveyors;

(f)

a representative of a civil society organisation with knowledge and experience in matters relevant to this Act; and

(g) one person from the private sector with knowledge and experience in matters relevant to this Act.

(2) The Director of the Road Transport and Safety Agency and the Director of the National Road Fund Agency shall be ex-officio members of the Board and shall have no vote.

(3) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.
