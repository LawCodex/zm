# The Patents and Companies Registration Agency (Amendment) Act, 2026

Patents and Companies Registration Agency (Amendment) [No. 68 of 2026

757

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 68 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Patents and Companies Registration Act [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1)This Act may be cited as the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Patents and Companies Registration Agency Act, 2020, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No.4 of 2020

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place: “Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants” means the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants established under the Accountants Act;.

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Agency which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative of the ministry responsible for commerce;

(b) a representative of the AttorneyGeneral;

(c) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants;

(d) a person with expertise in matters of intellectual property; and

(e) three persons from the private sector with knowledge and experience in matters relevant to this Act.;

(3) The members referred to under subsection
