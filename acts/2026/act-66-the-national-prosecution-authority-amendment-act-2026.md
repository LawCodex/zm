# The National Prosecution Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026

National Prosecution Authority (Amendment)

[No. 66 of 2026 753

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 66 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Prosecution Authority Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Prosecution Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Prosecution Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 105

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 7(1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (h) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(h) two persons from the private sector with knowledge and experience in prosecutorial matters;.
