# The Biosafety (Amendment)Act,2026

Biosafety (Amendment)

[No. 49 of 2026 691

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 49 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Biosafety Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Biosafety (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Biosafety Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap.151

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place: “legally disqualified” means the absence of legal capacity as provided in section 4 of the Mental Health Act.

## Section 3

The First Schedule to the principal Act is amended in—

(a) paragraph 1, by the deletion of subparagraphs (1) and (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:
