# The National Arts Council of Zambia Act, 2026

National Arts Council of Zambia (Amendment) [No. 30 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 30 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Arts Council of Zambia Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Arts Council of Zambia (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Arts Council of Zambia Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 170

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Council holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Council in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Law Association of Zambia” means the Law Association of Zambia established under the Law Association of Zambia Act; and “legally disqualified” means the absence of legal capacity as provided under section 4 of the Mental Health Act;.

## Section 3 — National Arts Council of Zambia (Amendment)

The Schedule to the principal Act is amended—

(a) by the deletion of paragraph 2 and the substitution therefor of the following: 2. (1) The Council consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each from the ministries responsible for—

(i) tourism;

(ii) commerce;

(iii) education; and

(iv) arts;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Law Association of Zambia;

(d) three representatives of the most representative associations engaged in matters relevant to the arts and creative industry; and

(e) two persons from the private sector with knowledge and experience in investment, resource mobilisation or strategic partnership development for the arts and creative industry.

(2) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

(3) The Chairperson and the ViceChairperson shall be elected by members of the Council from amongst themselves, subject to ratification by the Minister.

(4) A person shall not be appointed as a member of the Council if that person is—

(a) an undischarged bankrupt;

(b) legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member; or
