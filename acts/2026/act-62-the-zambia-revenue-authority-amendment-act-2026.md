GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 62 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Zambia Revenue Authority Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Revenue Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Governing Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Governing Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Emoluments Commission” means the Emoluments Commission established under the Constitution; and “legally disqualified” means the absence of legal capacity as provided under section 4 of the Mental Health Act;.

## Section 6A

(b) by the insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection (3):

(4) A person shall not be appointed as a member of the Governing Board if that person—

(a) is an undischarged bankrupt;

(b) is convicted of an offence involving fraud or dishonesty under any written law;

(c) is legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member; or

(d) has been convicted of an offence under this Act or any other written law and sentenced to imprisonment for a term exceeding six months without an option of a fine. 5.
