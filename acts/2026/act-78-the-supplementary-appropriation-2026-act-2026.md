# The Supplementary Appropriation (2026) Act, 2026

[No. 78 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 78 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026

An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2026, not exceeding in the aggregate twentysix billion, three hundred and twenty million, six hundred and ninety-six thousand, and five hundred and eighty-one kwacha. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation Short title

(2026) Act, 2026.

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, Supplementary there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies Appropriation not exceeding in the aggregate twenty-six billion, three hundred and twenty million, six hundred and ninety-six thousand, and five hundred and eighty-one kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2026.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K12.00 each.
