# The National Heritage Conservation Commission (Amendment) Act, 2026

National Heritage Conservation Commission [No. 32 of 2026 (Amendment)

641

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT

No. 32 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Heritage Conservation Commission Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Heritage Conservation Commission (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Heritage Conservation Commission Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 173

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Commission holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Commission in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order—

Amendment of section 2

“Emoluments Commission” means the Emoluments Commission established under the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“House of Chiefs” means the House of Chiefs established under the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“legally disqualified” means the absence of legal capacity as provided in section 4 of the Mental Health Act;

Cap. 305
