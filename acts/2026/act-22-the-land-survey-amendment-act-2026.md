# The Land Survey (Amendment) Act ,2026

Land Survey (Amendment)

[No. 22 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 22 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Land Survey Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Land Survey (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Land Survey Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and Commencement Cap. 188

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “House of Chiefs” means the House of Chiefs established under the Constitution; “Law Association of Zambia” means the Law Association of Zambia established under the Law Association of Zambia Act; “Zambia Institute of Estate Agents” means the Zambia Institute of Estate Agents established under the Estate Agents Act; and

## Section 3

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsections (1), (2), (3) and (4) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is established the Survey Control Board which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) the Chairperson, who shall be a registered land surveyor with knowledge and experience of at least ten years in land surveying in the Republic;

(b) a representative of the ministry responsible for lands;

(c) a representative of the Attorney- General;

(d) a representative of the House of Chiefs;

(e) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Planners;

(f) a representative of the Law Association of Zambia;

(g) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Estate Agents;

(h) a representative of the Surveyors Institute of Zambia;

(i) a representative of a civil society organisation dealing with customary land advocacy, environmental protection or gender;

(j) a representative of a religious organisation; and

(k) one person from the private sector with knowledge and experience in land management.

(2) The Surveyor-General shall be—

(a) an ex-officio member of the Board; and

(b) secretary to the Board.
