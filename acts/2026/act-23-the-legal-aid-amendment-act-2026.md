## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Legal Aid (Amendment) Short title and o mmen ce Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Legal Aid Act, 2021, in cment this Act referred to as the principal Act. Act No. 1 of 2021

## Section 3

Section 8 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(2) The Board consists of the following parttime members appointed by the Minister:

(a) the Chairperson, who shall be a person qualified to be a judge of the High Court;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Judiciary;

(d) a representative of the Zambia Law Development Commission;

(e) a representative of the Human Rights Commission;

(f) a representative of the Law Association of Zambia;

(g) two representatives from civil society organisations providing legal aid;

(h) a representative of a higher education institution, from the school of law, providing legal aid; and

(i) two persons from the private sector with knowledge and expertise in matters relating to this Act.

Amendment of First Schedule

## Section 4

Paragraph 4(4) of the First Schedule to the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “Six” and the substitution therefor of the word “Seven”.
