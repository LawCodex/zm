# The Competition and Consumer Protection Commission(Amendment)Act,2026

Competition and Consumer Protection (Amendment) [No. 48 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 48 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Competition and Consumer Protection Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Competition and Consumer Short title Protection (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with and commencement the Competition and Consumer Protection Act, in this Act referred Cap. 417 to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

The First Schedule to the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph 1 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of First Schedule

1\. (1) There is constituted the Board of the Commission which shall be the governing body of the Commission and responsible for the implementation of this Act.
