# The National HIV/STI/TB Council (Amendment)Act,2026

National HIV/AIDS/STI/TB Council (Amendment) [No. 50 of 2026 693

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 50 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National HIV/AIDS/STI/TB Council Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National HIV/AIDS/STI/ TB Council (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National HIV/AIDS/STI/TB Council Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 319

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Council holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Council in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 5 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement of section 5

## Section 5

(1) The Council consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each from the ministries responsible for—

(i) health;

(ii) finance;

(iii) local government; and

(iv) community development;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General; Single Copies of this Act can be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101, Lusaka, Price K4.00 each

(c) a representative of the Zambia Chamber of Commerce and Industry;

(d) a representative of a civil society organisation dealing with people living with HIV;

(e) a representative of a civil society organisation engaged in research and academia;

(f) a representative of a religious organisation;

(g) a representative of an association representing traditional, complementary and alternative medicines; and

(h) a representaive of a recognised youth organisation.

(2) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

(3) The Minister shall appoint the Chairperson from among the members of the Council.

(4) The members shall elect the ViceChairperson from among themselves.

Amendment of section 8

3\. Section 8(4) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “eight”and the substitution of the word “seven”.
