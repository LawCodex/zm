# The Civil Aviation Authority (Amendment) Act,2026

[No. 15 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 15 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Civil Aviation Authority Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Civil Aviation Authority (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Civil Aviation Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Amendment of section 2

(a) unlawful seizure of aircraft;

(b) destruction of aircraft in service;

(c) hostage-taking on board aircraft or at an aerodrome;

(f) use of an aircraft in service for the purpose of causing death, grievous harm or serious damage to property or the environment; or

## Section 7

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new section immediately after section 19:

Insertion of section 19A

19A. The Authority shall, in the regulation of civil aviation in the Republic, have the following primary purpose:

Primary purpose of Authority

(a) ensure the safety of passengers, crew, ground personnel and the general public in matters related to civil aviation; and

(b) ensure that civil aviation security remains the highest of priorities when making a decision relating to safety of passengers, crew, ground personnel and the general public.

## Section 9

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new sections immediately after section 33: 33A. (1) The Authority shall, in collaboration with the Immigration Department, establish an Advance Passenger Information System for purposes of—

(a) capturing passenger information prior to flight departure from, or flight arrival into, the Republic;

(b) transmitting and analysing the information referred to under paragraph (a) by relevant authorities; and

(c) enhancing law enforcement and security.

(2) The Advance Passenger Information System referred to under subsection (1) shall comply with the Zambia Civil Aviation Requirements relating to Passenger name record facilitation and relevant internationally recognised standards relating to advance passenger information.

(3) The Authority shall require aircraft operators operating in the Republic to submit advance passenger information through the Advance Passenger Information System in the manner and form as specified in the Zambia Civil Aviation Requirements.

Passenger name record

33B. (1) Subject to any other relevant written law, the Authority shall—

(a) in collaboration with the Immigration Department, develop capacity to collect, process, protect and use information relating to a passenger name record for purposes of—

(i) border control and national security;

(ii) risk assessment and analysis; and

(iii) identifying previously unknown persons who may be subjected to additional checks;

(b) align and manage information relating to a passenger name record with the Zambia Civil Aviation Requirements;

(c) identify information relating to a passenger name record for purposes of protecting civil aviation against an act of unlawful interference;

(d) set the purposes for which information relating to a passenger name record may be used by relevant authorities as the Authority may determine;

(e) disclose information relating to a passenger name record, as the Authority may determine, to relevant authorities in the Republic or other ICAO member States when exercising functions related to the processing of information relating to a passenger name record including purposes in respect of border security; and

(f) ensure that information relating to a passenger name record disclosed under paragraph (e) is protected by a relevant authority or ICAO member State in the same manner that the Authority protects that information. 33C. The Director-General shall issue an order, a notice, circular, directive, guidelines, procedures or requirements relating to the following:

Guidelines

(a) advance passenger information;

(b) the Advance Passenger Information System; and

(c) passenger name record.
