ACT No. 21 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Higher Educatoin Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Higher Education (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Higher Education Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 136

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

(d) a representative of the Examinations Council of Zambia;

(e) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Human Resource Management;

(f) a representative of the Technical Education, Vocational and Entrepreneurship Training Authority;
