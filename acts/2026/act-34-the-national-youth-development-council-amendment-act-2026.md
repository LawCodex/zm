# The National Youth Development Council (Amendment) Act, 2026

[No. 34 of 2026 647

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 34 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Youth Development Council Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Youth Development Council (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Youth Development Council Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 144

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Council holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Council in accordance with this Act.

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 5 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Composition of Council

## Section 5

(1) The Council consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each from the ministries responsible for —

(i) youth; and

(ii) finance;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Zambia Agency for Persons with Disabilities, who shall be a youth;

(d) two representatives from the most representative civil society organisations;

(e) three representatives from the most representative youth organisations; and

(f) two representatives from the private sector with knowledge and experience in matters relating to this Act.

(2) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

(3) The Minister shall appoint the Chairperson from among the members of the Council.

(4) The members shall elect the Vice-Chairperson from among themselves.

(5) A person shall not be appointed as a member of the Council if that person is —

(a) an undischarged bankrupt;

(b) legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member; or

(c) convicted of an offence under this Act or any other law and sentenced to a term of imprisonment exceeding six months without an option of a fine.

## Section 7

(1) The office of a member of the Council becomes vacant if the member —

(a) dies;

(b) is adjudged bankrupt under any written law;

(c) is absent, without reasonable excuse and without the prior approval of the Council, from three consecutive meetings of the Council, of which the member has had notice;

(d) resigns, by giving one month’s notice in writing, to the Minister;

(e) is legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member;

(f) ceases to be an employee or a member of the ministry, institution or organisation that nominated that member; or

(g) is convicted of an offence under this Act or any other written law and sentenced to imprisonment for a term exceeding six months without the option of a fine.

(2) The Minister shall, where the office of a member of the Council becomes vacant before the expiry of the term of office, appoint another member in place of that member of the Council but that member shall hold office as a member of the Council only for the unexpired part of the term of the Council.
