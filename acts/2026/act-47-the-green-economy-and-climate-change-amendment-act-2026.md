# The Green Economy and Climate Change(Amendment) Act,2026

Green Economy and Climate Change (Amendment) [No. 47 of 2026 687

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 47 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Green Economy and Climate Change Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Green Economy and Climate Change (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Green Economy and Climate Change Act, 2024, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Technical Committee holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Technical Committee in accordance with this Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 18 of 2024
