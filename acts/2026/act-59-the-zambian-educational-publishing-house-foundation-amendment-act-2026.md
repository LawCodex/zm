## Section 4A

(1) A member of the Foundation shall, subject to other provisions of this Act, hold office for a term of three years from the date of appointment and may be re-appointed for a further and final term of three years.

(2) A member shall, on the expiration of the term for which the member is appointed, continue to hold office until another member is appointed, but in no case shall an extension of the period exceed three months.

(3) The office of a member of the Foundation becomes vacant if the member—

(a) dies;

(b) is adjudged bankrupt under any written law;

(c) is absent, without reasonable excuse and without the prior approval of the Foundation from three consecutive meetings of the Foundation of which the member has had notice;

(d) resigns, by giving one month’s notice, in writing, to the Minister;

(e) is legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member of the Foundation;

(f) ceases to be an employee of the ministry, institution or organisation that nominated that member; or

(g) is convicted of an offence under this Act or any other written law and sentenced to imprisonment for a term exceeding six months without the option of a fine.

(4) The Minister shall, where the office of a member of the Foundation becomes vacant before the expiry of the term of office of a member, appoint another member in place of that member, but the member shall hold office as a member only for the unexpired part of the term of the Foundation.
