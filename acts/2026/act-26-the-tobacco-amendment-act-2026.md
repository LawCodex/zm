# The Tobacco (Amendment) Act, 2026

Tobacco (Amendment)

[No. 26 of 2026 625

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 26 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Tobacco Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Tobacco (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Tobacco Act, 2022, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 10 of 2022

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsections (1) and (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Tobacco Board of Zambia which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i) agriculture;

(ii) commerce; and

(iii) finance;

(b)

a representative of the AttorneyGeneral;
