# The Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment) Act,2026

Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment)

[No. 55 of 2026 709

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 55 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Zambia National Public Health Institute Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Zambia National Public Health Institute Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 19 of 2020

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101, Lusaka, Price K4.00 each

710 No. 55 of 2026] Amendment of section 5

Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment)

## Section 2

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Institute which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for —

(i)

health;

(ii) animal health;

(iii) agriculture;

(iv) water and sanitation; and

(v)

local government;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a representative of the Disaster Management and Mitigation Unit;

(d) a representative of the Occupational Health and Safety Institute;

(e) a representative of a civil society organisation engaged in public health;

(f) a representative of academia from the private sector with knowledge and experience in matters relating to this Act; and

(g) one person from the private sector with knowledge and experience in public health or a related field. Amendment of First Schedule

## Section 3

Paragraph 3(4) of the First Schedule to the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “Six” and the substitution therefor of the word “Seven”.
