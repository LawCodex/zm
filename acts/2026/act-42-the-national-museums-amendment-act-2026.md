# The National Museums(Amendment)Act,2026

National Museums (Amendment)

[No. 42 of 2026 673

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 42 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Museums Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Museums (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Museums Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap.172

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order:

Amendment of section 2

“House of Chiefs” means the House of Chiefs established under the Constitution;

Cap.1

“Zambia Institute of Marketing” means the Zambia Institute of Marketing established under the Zambia Institute of Marketing Act, 2022.

## Section 3 — National Museums (Amendment)

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) The Board consists of the following parttime members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i)

culture; and

(ii) finance;

(b) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(c) a chief nominated by the House of Chiefs;

(d) a representative of the Tourism Council of Zambia;

(e) a representative of the Zambia Institute of Marketing;

(f) a representative of an institution undertaking research in matters relevant to this Act;

(g) a representative of the University of Zambia and Allied Workers Union; and

(h) one person with knowledge and experience in matters relevant to this Act.;

(b) insertion of the following new subsections immediately after subsection (1):

(2) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

(3) The Minister shall appoint the Chairperson from among the members of the Board.; and

(c) Amendment of section 7

renumbering of subsections (2), (3) and (4) as subsections (4), (5) and (6), respectively.

## Section 4

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) Five members shall form a quorum at a meeting of the Board.
