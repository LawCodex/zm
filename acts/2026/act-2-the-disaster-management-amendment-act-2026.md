This Act may be cited as the Disaster Management (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Disaster Management Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

### Section 5

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 5 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement of section 5

5\. (1) The Division shall be responsible for implementing disaster risk management programmes and activities in the Republic.

(2) Despite the generality of subsection (1), the functions of the Division are to—

(a) act as the central planning, coordinating and monitoring institution, taking into account potential disaster risks, for—

(i) the prevention and mitigation of a disaster;

(ii) the preparedness and response to a disaster; and

(iii) post-disaster recovery;

(b) advise the Technical Committee on matters relating to disaster risk management and management of disaster relief operations;

(c) warn the public of an imminent disaster and predict the effects of the disaster;

Functions of Division

(d) maintain a data collection and dissemination system, national strategic reserves of essential commodities and equipment, for purposes of immediate disaster relief;

(e) formulate disaster prevention, mitigation, preparedness, response and rehabilitation strategies and action plans to meet foreseeablere quirements in collaboration with Government agencies, non-governmental organisations, donor agencies and other cooperating partners;

(f) prepare and update disaster risk management plans and the supporting disaster risk management manual;

(g) prepare an over-arching national disaster contingency plan and coordinate cross sectoral disaster contingency plans;

(h) establish an early warning system covering all sectors and hazard sources and maintain close links with different institutions that provide early warning services;

(i) develop and sustain viable and effective structures and capacities at national, provincial, district and satellite level;

(j)take necessary measures in order to prevent, alleviate, contain and minimise the effects of a disaster;

(k) conduct public and media briefings on risks and disaster related programmes, progress and constraints;

(l) act as an advisory and consultative body on issues relating to disasters and disaster risk management;

(m) develop post-disaster reconstruction, rehabilitation and recovery plans in collaboration with relevant stakeholders;

(n) promote the recruitment, training and participation of volunteers in disaster risk management;

(o) establish and manage a national emergency operations centre, which shall be—

(i) the nerve centre to monitor hazards, risks and emergencies; and

(ii) linked to the normal operations of the Division;

(p) act as a central clearing house for the reporting of a disaster and dissemination of information from the Council and Technical Committee before, during and after a disaster;

(q) conduct comprehensive vulnerability and risk assessments before, during and after a disaster;

(r) collaborate with other institutions and organisations which are responsible for disaster risk management at national, regional and international level;

(s) promote the integration of disaster risk reduction and climate change adaptation for purposes of enhancing capacities and strengthening resilience of communities; and

(t) maintain, and cause to be maintained, adequate response capacity and equipment to facilitate timely, effective and efficient response to a recurrent and anticipated disaster including—

(i) ground and aerial assessment and analysis tools for capturing spatial and digital data and images;

(ii) ground and aerial transportation and haulage equipment sufficient for timely and efficient dispatch of supplies to a disaster affected area;

(iii) ground and aerial transportation equipment for timely and efficient dispatch of emergency response teams, equipment and materials;
