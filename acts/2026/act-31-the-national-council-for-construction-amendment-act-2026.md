ACT No. 31 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Council for Construction Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Council for Construction (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Council for Construction Act, 2020, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Enactment Short title and commencement Act No. 10 of 2020

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.
