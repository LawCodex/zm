# The Nurses and Midwives (Amendment) Act, 2026

Nurses and Midwives (Amendment)

[No. 35 of 2026 651

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 35 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Nurses and Midwives Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Nurses and Midwives (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Nurses and Midwives Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 300

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in subsection (1), by the deletion of paragraph (e) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 5

(e) a representative from the private sector with knowledge and experience in finance or business management related to the nursing and midwifery profession;

(b)

by the insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection (1):

(2) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.; and

(c) by the renumbering of subsections (2), (3) and (4) as subsections (3), (4) and (5), respectively.
