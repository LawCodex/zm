# The National Road Fund(Amendment)Act,2026

National Road Fund (Amendment)

[No. 38 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT

No. 38 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Road Fund Act. [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Road Fund (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Road Fund Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Agency holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Agency in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport” means the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport established under the Zambia Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport Act; and “Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants” means the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants established under the Accountants Act.

## Section 3

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) The Agency consists of the following parttime members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each from the ministries responsible for—

(i) finance;

(ii) transport; and

(iii) infrastructure development;
