# The Environmental Management (Amendment) Act , 2026

Environmental Management (Amendment) [No. 27 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 27 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Environmental Management Act [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Environmental Management (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Environmental Management Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 204

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Engineering Institution of Zambia” means the Engineering Institution of Zambia established under the Engineering Institution of Zambia Act; “Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants” means the Zambia Institute of Chartered Accountants established under the Accountants Act; and “Zambia Institute of Planners” means the Zambia Institute of Planners established under the Urban and Regional Planners Act.
