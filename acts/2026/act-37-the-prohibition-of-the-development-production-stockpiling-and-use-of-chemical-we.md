# The Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and use of Chemical Weapons (Amendment) Act, 2026

Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons [No. 37 of 2026 657 (Amendment)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 37 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Prohibition of the Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 472

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Authority holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Authority in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place in alphabetical order: “Zambia Revenue Authority” means the Zambia Revenue Authority established under the Zambia Revenue Authority Act.
