# The Zambia Wildlife(Amendment) Act,2026

[No. 54 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 54 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Zambia Wildlife Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia Wildlife (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Wildlife Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 201

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Committee holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Committee in accordance with this Act.

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order:

## Section 3 — Zambia Wildlife (Amendment)

Section 7 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsections (2), (3), (4) and (5) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(2) The Committee consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i) chiefs;

(ii) green economy; and

(iii) mines;

(b) a representative of the Office of the President;

(c) a representative of the Attorney-General;

(d) a representative of the Anti-Corruption Commission;

(e) a representative each from an association responsible for—

(i) the hunting industry; and

(ii) tourism operators;

(f) a representative of the Wildlife Producers Association of Zambia;

(g) a representative of a civil society organisation with knowledge and experience in matters relating to tourism and wildlife; and

(h) one person from the private sector with relevant knowledge and experience in matters relating to this Act.

(3) The Director shall be an ex-officio member of the Committee.

(4) The ministries, institutions and organisations referred to in subsection (1) shall nominate their representatives for appointment by the Minister.

[No. 54 of 2026

(5) The Minister shall appoint the Chairperson from among the members of the Committee.;

(b) insertion of the following new subsections immediately after subsection (5):

(6) The members of the Committee shall elect the Vice-Chairperson from among themselves.

(7) A person shall not be appointed as a member of the Committee if that person—

(a) is not a citizen;

(b) is an undischarged bankrupt;

(c) is legally disqualified from performing the functions of a member; or

(d) has been convicted of an offence under this Act or any other written law and sentenced to imprisonment for a term exceeding six months without an option of a fine.; and

(c) renumbering of subsections (6) and (7) as subsections (8) and (9), respectively.
