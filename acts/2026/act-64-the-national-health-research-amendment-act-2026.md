# The National Health Research (Amendment) Act, 2026

National Health Research (Amendment)

[No. 64 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 64 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the National Health Research Act. [ 12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the National Health Research (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the National Health Research Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 318

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Council holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Council in accordance with this Act.
