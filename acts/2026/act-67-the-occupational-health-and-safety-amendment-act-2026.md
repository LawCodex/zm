# The Occupational Health and Safety (Amendment) Act, 2026

Occupational Health and Safety (Amendment) [No. 67 of 2026

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 67 of 2026 Date of Assent: 4th June, 2026 An Act to amend the Occupational Health and Safety Act [12th June, 2026 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Occupational Health and Safety (Amendment) Act, 2026, and shall be read as one with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, 2025, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 16 of 2025

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the expiry of the term of office, or removal by the Minister, of the members of the Board holding office prior to the enactment of this Act, after which the Minister shall appoint the members of the Board in accordance with this Act.

## Section 2

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 6

(1) There is constituted the Board of the Institute which consists of the following part-time members appointed by the Minister:

(a) a representative each of the ministries responsible for—

(i) health;

(ii) labour; and

(iii) mines; Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K4.00 each.
