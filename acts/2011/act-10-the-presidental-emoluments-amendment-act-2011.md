# The Presidental (Emoluments) (Amendment) Act, 2011

THE PRESIDENTIAL (EMOLUMENTS) (AMENDMENT) BILL, 2011 MEMORADUM The object of this Bill is to amend the Presidential (Emoluments) Act so as to—

(a) revise the criterion for payment of gratuity to a person who has held the office of President; and

(b) provide for matters connected with, or incidental to, the foregoing.

A. SHONGA, JNR, Attorney-General

N.A.B. 11, 2011 22nd Februaru, 2011

A BILL ENTITLED

An Act to amend the Presidential (Emoluments) Act. [ ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Presidential (Emoluments) (Amendment) Act, 2011, and shall be read as one with the Presidential (Emoluments) Act, in this Act 5 referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section three and the substitution therefor of the following:

## Section 3

(1) A person who has held the office of President shall, upon the expiration of a term of office 10 or upon ceasing to be President, be paid a gratuity equal to one hundred percent of the aggregate amount of emoluments paid or payable to the person in respect of the period spent in office.

(2) Where a person who holds office of President ceases to hold office— 15

(a) by reason of death; or

(b) when a new President assumes office after an election following the dissolution of Parliament before its full term; 20 that person shall be deemed to have served as President for the purpose of this section. N.A.B. 11, 2011
