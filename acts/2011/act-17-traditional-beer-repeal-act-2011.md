# Traditional Beer (Repeal) Act 2011

The Traditional Beer (Repeal)

[No. 17 of /2011 225

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 17 of 2011

Date of Assent: 12th April, 2011

An Act to repeal the Traditional Beer Act, 1930

[15th April, 2011 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Traditional Beer (Repeal) Act, 2011.

Short title

(1) The Traditional Beer Act, 1930, is hereby repealed.

:Repeal of Cap. 168

(2) Notwithstanding subsection (1), a person who immediately Act No. 11 of 1930 before the commencement of this Act, held a permit to manufacture, process or sell traditional beer shall hold such permit for a period of three months, or for such extended period as the Minister may determine, after which that person shall apply for a liquor licence under the provisions of the Liquor Licensing Act, 2011. ; ;Act No. 20 nIsf. 2011
