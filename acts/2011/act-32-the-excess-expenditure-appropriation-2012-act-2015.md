GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 10 of 2015 Date of Assent: 14th August, 2015 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of monies aggregating twelve million, seven hundred and eighty-five thousand, thirty-nine kwacha. [ 14th August, 2015 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the The Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2012) Act, 2015.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year which ended on 31st December, 2012 of the monies indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2011, and the Supplementary Appropriation (2012) Act, 2014, is approved.
