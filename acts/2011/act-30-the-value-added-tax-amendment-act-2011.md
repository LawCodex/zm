# The Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act (2011)

Value Added Tax (Amendment)

[No. 30 of 2011 519

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 30 of 2011

Date of Assent: 28th December, 2011

An Act to amend the Value Added Tax Act. [29th December, 2011 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2011, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Enactment Short title and commencement Cap. 331

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2012.

## Section 2

Section two of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “ accounting year” and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 2

“ accounting year” means a period of twelve months ending on 31st December or such other period as the CommissionerGeneral may prescribe.”
