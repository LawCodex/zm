# The Zambia Development Agency (Amendment) Act (2011)

Zambia Development Agency (Amendment)

[No. 29 of 2011 517

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 29 of 2011

Date of Assent: 28th December, 2011 An Act to amend the Zambia Development Agency Act, 2006. [29th December, 2011 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Development Agency Short title (Amendment) Act, 2011, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Development Agency Act, 2006, in this Act referred to as the Act No. 11 of 2006 principal Act.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section fiftyeight.

Repeal of section 58
