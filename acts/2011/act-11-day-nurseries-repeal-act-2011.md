# Day_Nurseries_(Repeal) Act, 2011

Day Nurseries (Repeal)

[ No. 11 of 2011 85

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 11 of 2011 Date ofAssent: 12th April, 2011 An Act to repeal the Day Nurseries Act. [18th Apri1,2011 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Day Nurseries (Repeal) Act,

Short title

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

Interpretation

" repealed Act "means the Day Nurseries Act, 1957.

Act No. 63 of 1957 Repeal of Cap 313

2011.

3\. (1) The Day Nurseries Act, 1957, is hereby repealed. Notwithstanding subsection (1), a certificate of registration issued under the repealed Act shall be valid for a period of six months after which the holder thereof shall apply for registration in accordance with the provisions of the Education Act, 2011. Notwithstanding subsection (1), any existing rights conferred on a person under the repealed Act shall continue to be held by the person as if conferred under the Education Act, 2011, and for the purposes of the Education Act, 2011. 4. (1) Any legal proceedings pending immediately before the publication of this Act by or against a local authority may be continued by or against the Government.
