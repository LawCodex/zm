# Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2024

Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) [No. 26 of 2024

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 26 of 2024 Date of Assent: 20th December, 2024 An Act to amend the Zambia Revenue Authority Act. [26th December, 2024 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2024, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Revenue Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2)

Enactment Short title and commencement Cap. 321

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2025.

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 18 and the substitution therefor of the following: 18. An action or other proceedings shall not lie or be instituted against a member, a member of a committee or a member of staff of the Authority for, or in respect of, an act or thing done or omitted to be done in good faith in the exercise or performance of any of the powers, functions or duties conferred under this Act.

Repeal and replacement of section 18 Immunity of members and staff
