# The Judiciary Administration (Amendment) Act, 2024

Judiciary Administration (Amendment)

[No. 16 of 2024

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 16 of 2024 Date of Assent: 20th December, 2024 An Act to amend the Judiciary Administration Act, 2016. [26th December, 2024 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Judiciary Administration (Amendment) Act, 2024, and shall be read as one with the Judiciary Administration Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Act No. 23 of 2016

## Section 2

Section 23 (2) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraph (g).

Amendment of section 23

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K 4.00 each.
