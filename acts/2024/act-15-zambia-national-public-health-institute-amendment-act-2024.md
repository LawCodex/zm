# Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment) Act, 2024

Zambia National Public Health Institute [No. 15 of 2024 (Amendment)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 15 of 2024 Date of Assent: 20th December, 2024 An Act to amend the Zambia National Public Health Institute Act, 2020. [26th December, 2024 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia National Public Health Institute (Amendment) Act, 2024, and shall be read as one with the Zambia National Public Health Institute Act, 2020, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place: “National Health Research and Training Institute” means the National Health Research and Training Institute established under the National Research and Training Institute Act, 2024;.

## Section 3

Section 4 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) insertion of the figure “(1)” immediately after the figure “4”;

(b) deletion of paragraph (p) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(p) conduct research, to ensure public health security and global health security;; and

(c) insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection (1):

(2) The research referred to under subsection (1)

(p) shall be conducted in accordance with the National Health Research Act, 2013.
