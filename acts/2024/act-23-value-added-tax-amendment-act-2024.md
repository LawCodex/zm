GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 23 of 2024 Date of Assent: 20th December, 2024 An Act to amend the Value Added Tax Act. [26th December, 2024 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2024, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 331

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2025.
