# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (1999)

Supplement to the Republic of Zambia Gazette dated 20th April. 2004

Government [No. 4 of 2004

23

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 4 of 2004 Date of Assent: 15th April, 2004 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of the moneys aggregating fifty-five billion, sixty-six million, forty-seven thousand, three hundred and eighty-three Kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year en ded 31 st December, 1999. [20th April, 2004 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation

(1999) Act, 2004.

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 1999, of the moneys indicated in respect of the services in the. third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for services by the Appropriation Act, 1999 and the Supplementary Appropriation (1999) Act, 2002, is hereby approved.
