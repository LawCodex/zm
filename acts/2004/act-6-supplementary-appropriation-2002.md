Appropriation

(2000)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2004 Date of Assent: 15th April, 2004 An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating one hundred sixty-six billion, eight hundred fifty-seven million, six hundred eighty-seven thousand, eight hundred and fifty-three kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended 31st December, 2002. [20th April, 2004 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

1 . This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2002) Act, 2004.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such moneys indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Appropriation Act, 2002.

Supplementary appropriation

Copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, PO. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K1000 each.
