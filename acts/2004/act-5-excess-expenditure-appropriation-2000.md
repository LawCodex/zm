# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2000)

Excess Expenditure

[No. 5 of 2004 27

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 5 of 2004 Date of Assent: 15th April, 2004 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of the moneys aggregating sixty-eight billion, two hundred fifty-one million, one hundred twenty three thousand, five hundred and fifty-three kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended 31st December, 2000. [20th April, 2004 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess E x p e n d i t u r e Appropriation (2000) Act, 2004.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2000, of the moneys indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for services by the Appropriation Act, 2000 and the Supplementary Appropriation (2000) Act, 2002, is hereby approved.

Excess expenditure approved

Copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K1000 each.
