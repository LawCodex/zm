# High Court (Amendment)

High Court (Amendment)

[No. 16 of 2002

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 16 of 2002 Date of Assent: 31st December, 2002 An Act to amend the High Court Act [31st December, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the High Court (Amendment) Act, 2002, and shall be read as one with the High Court Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Cap. 27

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended in section ten, by—

(a) the deletion of the full stop at the end of the section and the substitution therefor of a colon; and

(b) the insertion after the section of the following new proviso: Provided that the Civil Court Practice 1999 (The Green Book) of England or any other civil court practice rules issued after 1999 in England shall not apply to Zambia unless they relate to matrimonial causes.
