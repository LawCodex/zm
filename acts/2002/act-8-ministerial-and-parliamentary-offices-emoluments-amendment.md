GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 8 of 2002

Date of Assent: 19th November, 2002 An Act to amend the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) Act [20th November, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) (Amendment) Act, 2002, and shall be read as one with the Ministerial and Parliamentary Offices (Emoluments) Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1st April, 2002.

Short title and commencement

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of the First and Second Schedules and the substitution therefor of the First and Second Schedules set out in the Appendix.

Repeal and replacement of the First and Second Schedules
