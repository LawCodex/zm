# Mines and Minerals (Amendment)

Mines and Minerals (Amendment)

[No. 5 of 2002 71

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 5 of 2002 Date of Assent: 29th March, 2002 An Act to Amend the Mines and Minerals Act [30th March, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

1 . ( 1 ) This Act may be cited as the Mines and Minerals (Amendment) Act, 2002, and shall be read as one with the Mines and Minerals Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the 1st April, 2002.

Short title and commencement Cap.213

## Section 2

Section two of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition:

Amendment of Section 2

" Former Zambia Consolidated Copper Mines Limited " means any mining division or metal treatment operation of Zambia Consolidated Copper Mines Limited sold under the Privatisation Act.

## Section 3

Section sixty-six of the principal Act is amended in subsection

(1) by the deletion of full the stop after the words " two per centum " and the insertion of the words " except in the case of the former Zambia Consolidated Copper Mining Company where the rate shall be zero point six per centum " .
