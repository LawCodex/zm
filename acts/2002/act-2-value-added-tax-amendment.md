# Value Added Tax (Amendment)

Value Added Tax (Amendment)

[No. 2 of 2002

55

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 2 of 2002 Date of Assent: 29th March, 2002 An Act to Amend the Value Added Tax Act [30th March, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2002, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

(2) This Act shall come into operation on the 1st April, 2002.

Title and commencement Cap.331

## Section 2

Section eighteen of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (4) and the substitution therefor of the following new subsection:

Amendment of section 18

(4) Input tax may not be deducted or credited after a period of one year from the date of the relevant tax invoice or other evidence referred to in subsection (3), except in such circumstances as may be allowed by administrative rules made by the Commissioner-General.

## Section 3

Section twenty of the principal Act is amended by—

(a) the deletion of subsection (2), and

Amendment of section 20

(b) the re-numbering of subsections (3) and (4) as (2), and

(3) respectively.
