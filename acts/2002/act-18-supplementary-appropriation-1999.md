# Supplementary Appropriation (1999)

[No. 18 of 2002 361

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 18 of 2002 Date of Assent: 31st December, 2002 An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating one hundred and sixty-nine billion, two hundred and eighty-three million, three hundred and nineteen thousand three hundred and fiftynine kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 3 1 December, 1999. st

[31stDecember,2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(1999) Act, 2002.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that, there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such moneys indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated, for the services of the Republic, by the Appropriation Act, 1999.
