# Supplementary Appropriation (2000)

Appropriation

(2000)

[No. 19 of

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

No. 19 of 2002

Date of Assent: 31st December, 2002 An Act to confirm the Supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating two hundred and ninety-nine billion, four hundred million, seven hundred and eleven thousand, two hundred and twelve kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2000. [31st December, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2000) Act, 2002.

Short title

## Section 2

On the Authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that, there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such moneys indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated, for the services of the Republic, by the Appropriation Act, 2000.

Supplementary appropriation
