# Presidential Emoluments

Supplement to the Republic of Zambia Government Gazette dated Friday. 20th November. 2002

[No. 7 of2002 87

GOVERNMENTOFZAMBIA

ACT No. 7 of 2002

Date of Assent: 19th November, 2002 An Act to amend the Presidential Emoluments Act [20th November, 2002 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Presidential Emoluments (Amendment) Act, 2002, and shall be read as one with the Presidential Emoluments Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 261

(2) This Act shall be deemed to have come into operation on the 1 st April, 2002.

## Section 2

Section two of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of paragraphs (a) and (b) and the substitution therefor of the following new paragraphs:

(a) a salary calculated at the annual rate of K54,437,388; and

(b) a special annual allowance of K44,373,864.
