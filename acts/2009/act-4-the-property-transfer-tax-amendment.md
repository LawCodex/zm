# The Property Transfer Tax (Amendment)

The Property Transfer Tax

[No. 4 of 2009 33

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 4 of 2009 Date of Assent: 31 March, 2009 An Act to amend the Property Transfer Tax Act. [ 3rd April, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

(1) This Act may be cited as the Property Transfer Tax (Amendment) Act, 2009, and shall be read as one with the Property Transfer Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Enactment Short title and commencement Cap. 340

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st April, 2009. Section five of the principal Act is amended in subsection

(2) by—

Amendment of section 5

the deletion of the full stop at the end of that subsection and the substitution therefor of a colon: and the insertion o lthe following new proviso: Provided that the Commissioner General, in determining the realised value for shares, may make adjustments in accordance with the provisions of sections ninety-sevenA, ninety-sevenAA, ninety-sevenC and paragraph 22A of the Fifth Schedule to the Income Tax Act.
