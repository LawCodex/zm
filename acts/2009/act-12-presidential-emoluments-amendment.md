# Presidential Emoluments (Amendment)

Presidential Emoluments (Amendment)

[No. 12 of 2009 71

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 12 of 2009

Date of Assent: 11th August, 2009 An Act to amend the Presidential Emoluments Act.

112th August, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Presidential Emoluments (Amendment) Act, 2009, and shall be read as one with the Presidential Emoluments Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section two and the substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement

Cap. 261

section 2

## Section 2

(1) The President shall be paid a salary and allowances at such rate as the Minister responsible for finance may prescribe, by statutory instrument, in consultation with the Standing Orders Committee of the National Assembly.

(2) For the avoidance of doubt, the President shall continue to receive the salary and allowances applicable until the coming into operation of such statutory instrument as the Minister responsible for finance may prescribe under subsection (1).
