Excess Expenditure Appropriation

[No. 6 of 2009

G O V E R N M E N T OF ZAMBIA

No. 6 of 2009 Date of Assent: 31 st March, 2009 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of moneys aggregating nine billion, three hundred and fifty nine million, one hundred and fifty one thousand, nine hundred and forty seven kwacha required for the services of the R e p u b l i c d u r i n g the financial year ended on 31st December, 2006. [3rd April, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2006) Act, 2009.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year which ended on 31 st December, 2006, of the moneys indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2006, and the Supplementary Appropriation (2006) Act, 2008, is hereby approved,
