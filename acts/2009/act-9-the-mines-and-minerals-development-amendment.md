# The Mines and Minerals Development (Amendment)

Mines and Minerals Development (Amendment)

[No. 9 of 2009 51

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 9 of 2009 Date of Assent: 31st March, 2009

An Act to amend the Mines and Minerals Development Act.

[3rd April, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Mines and Minerals Development (Amendment) Act, 2009, and shall be read as one with the Mines and Minerals Development Act, 2008, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short tale

Section seven of the principal Act is amended by— the deletion of subsection (4); and

Act No. 7 of 2008 Amendment of section 7

the re-numbering of subsections (4) and (5) respectively.
