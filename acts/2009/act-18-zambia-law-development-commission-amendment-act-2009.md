# Zambia Law Development Commission (Amendment) Act 2009

Zambia Law Development Commission (Amendment) [No. 18 of 2009 209 GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 18 of 2009 Date of Assent: 28th August, 2009 An Act to amend the Zambia Law Development Commission Act. [ 31st August, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia. This Act may be cited as the Zambia Law Development Commission (Amendment) Act, 2009 and shall be read as one with the Zambia Law Development Commission Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act. Section five of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) The Commission shall consist of the following part time members: a judge nominated by the Chief justice; a representative of the Law School at a public university, nominated by the Minister responsible for education; a representative of the Law School at a private university, nominated by the Minister responsible for education; a representative of a research institute, nominated by the Minister responsible for education; the Permanent Secretary responsible for legal matters in the Minister responsible for justice; a representative of the Attorney General with expertise in legislative drafting;
