# Notaries Public and Notorial Functions (Amendment) Act 2009

Notaries Public and Notarial Functions [No. 23 of 2009 347 (Amendment)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 23 of 2009

Date of Assent: 28th August,2009 An Act to amend the Notaries Public and Notarial Functions Act. [ 31st August, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Notaries Public and Notarial Functions (Amendment) Act, 2009, and shall be read as one with

Short title

the Notaries Public and Notarial Functions Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Cap. 35

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section thirtyone and the substitution therefor of the following new section: 31. (1) The Registrar shall, upon the grant to any legal practitioner of Letters Patent of the rank and dignity of State Counsel for Zambia, cause the name of the legal practitioner to be entered on the Roll of Notaries Public. A person who, on the commencement of this Act, enjoys the rank and dignity of State Counsel for Zambia, shall be deemed to have been appointed and enrolled as notary public under, and for the purposes of, this Act. The Registrar shall cause the name of a person referred to in subsection (2) to be entered on the Roll of Notaries Public upon the commencement of this Act.

Copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, PO. Box 30136, Lusaka. Price K3000 each.
