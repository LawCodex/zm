# The Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2006)

Excess Expenditure Appropriation

[No. 6 of 2009 39

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2009 Date of Assent: 31st March, 2009 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of moneys aggregating nine billion, three hundred and fifty nine million, one hundred and fifty one thousand, nine hundred and forty seven kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2006.

[3rd April, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2006) Act, 2009.

Short title

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the financial year which ended on 31st December, 2006, of the moneys indicated in respect of the services in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services by the Appropriation Act, 2006, and the Supplementary Appropriation (2006) Act, 2008, is hereby approved.

Excess expenditure approval

Copies of this Act can be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K1,000 each.
