# Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2005)

Excess Expenditure Appropriation

[No. 6 of 2008 55

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2008 Date of Assent: 27th March, 2008 An Act to approve the excess expenditure of moneys aggregating eighty-three billion, three hundred and forty-one million, one hundred and thirty-five thousand, one hundred and seventy-seven kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year which ended on 31st December, 2005. (4th April, 2008 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

1 . This Act may be cited as the Excess Expenditure Appropriation (2005) Act, 2008.

Short title

## Section 2

The expenditure on the services of the Republic during the
