# The Supplementary Appropriation (2018) Act, 2018

[No. 10 of 2018

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 10 of 2018 Date of Assent: 28th July, 2018 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December 2018, not exceeding in the aggregate seven billion, two hundred and thirty-three million, five hundred and thirteen thousand, eight hundred and sixty-six kwacha. [31st July, 2018 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2018) Act, 2018.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not exceeding in the aggregate seven billion, two hundred and thirtythree million, five hundred and thirteen thousand, eight hundred and sixty-six kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2018.

Supplementary appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K8.00 each.
