# The Rent (Amendment) Act, 2018

The Rent (Amendment)

[No. 3 of 2018

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 3 of 2017 Date of Assent: 9th April, 2018 An Act to amend the Rent Act. [11th April, 2018 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Rent (Amendment) Act, 2018, and shall be read as one with the Rent Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short Title

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “Court” and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 2

Cap. 206

“court” means—

(a) in relation to premises for which the rent demanded exceeds one hundered thousand kwacha per year, the High Court; and

(b) in relation to all other permises, a subordinate court of the first class;
