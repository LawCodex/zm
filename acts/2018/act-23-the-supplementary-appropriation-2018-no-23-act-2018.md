# The Supplementary Appropriation (2018) (No. 23) Act, 2018

[No. 23 of 2018 451

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 23 of 2018 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2018 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2018, not exceeding in the aggregate three billion, five hundred and seventy-seven million, forty-eight thousand, three hundred and sixty-five kwacha. [26th December, 2018 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2018) (No. 2) Act, 2018.

Short title

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not exceeding in the aggregate three billion, five hundred and seventy-seven million, forty-eight thousand, three hundred and sixtyfive kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the First Schedule and Second Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2018.

Supplementary Appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K8.00 each.
