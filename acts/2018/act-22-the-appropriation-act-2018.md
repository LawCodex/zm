# The Appropriation Act, 2018

[No. 22 of 2018

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 22 of 2018 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2018 An Act to authorise expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2019, not exceeding in the aggregate eighty-six billion, eight hundred and seven million, eight hundred and ninetyfour thousand, seven hundred and twenty-seven kwacha. [26th December, 2018 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Appropriation Act, 2018.

## Section 2

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies, not exceeding in the aggregate eighty-six billion, eight hundred and seven million, eight hundred and ninety-four thousand, seven hundred and twenty-seven kwacha, as may be required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2019.

Enactment Short title Appropriation

(2) The monies referred to in subsection (1) shall be appropriated to the services of the Republic specified in the second column of both the First Schedule and the Second Schedule, in amounts not exceeding the amounts indicated in the third column of both the First Schedule and the Second Schedule.

(3) On the written authority of the Secretary to the Treasury, the amounts shown under the sub-heads in the First Schedule and under the programmes in the Second Schedule, may be varied, except that the amounts appropriated to each sub-head in the First Schedule and the amounts appropriated to each programme in the Second Schedule shall not be altered by the variation.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K44.00 each.
