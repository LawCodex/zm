# Customs and Excise (Amendment) No. 14 of 2017

[No. 14 of 2017 643

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 14 of 2017 Date of Assent:19th December, 2017 An Act to amend the Customs and Excise Act. [22nd December, 2017 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

### Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Customs and Excise (Amendment) Act, 2017, and shall be read as one with the Customs and Excise Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 322

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2018.

### Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following definition in the appropriate place:

Amendment of section 2

“ used motor vehicle ” means a motor vehicle that is at least two years old and which has been previously registered before importation.

### Section 3

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (4).

Amendment of section 3

### Section 4

The principal Act is amended by the deletion of section 72 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 72

72\. Subject to section 79, there shall be charged, levied, collected, and paid in respect of goods imported into Zambia, customs duties at the rates specified in the customs tariff set out in the First Schedule, in this Act referred to as the customs tariff, except that the rates for used motor vehicles shall be as specified in the Third Schedule. Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K40.00 each.

Amendment of section 76A

Customs and Excise (Amendment)

### Section 5

The principal Act is amended by the deletion of section 76A and the substitution therefor of the following: 76A. There shall, in addition to such other duties imposed by this Act or any other law, be charged, levied collected and paid excise duties at the rates specified in the excise tariff, in respect of any imported goods that are of a kind or class subject to excise duties in terms of section 76, except that the rates for used motor-vehicles shall be as specified in the Third Schedule.

Amendment of section 85

### Section 6

Section 85 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (3) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(3) Despite subsection (1), the customs value for a used motor vehicle shall be determined based on the valuation method specified in Clause 8 of the Fifth Schedule.; and

(b) insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection 4:

(5) The Commissioner-General may apply valuation reference materials to test declared customs values.

Amendment of section 188

### Section 7

Section 188 of the principal Act is amended by the —

(a) deletion of subsection (5) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(5) A person commits an offence if that person—

(a) avails incomplete records or fails to avail the records for inspection as required under this section; or

(b) fails to provide information requested by an authorised officer within the time stipulated by that officer.; and

(b) insertion of the following new subsections immediately after subsection (5):

(5A) A person convicted of an offence under subsection (5) is liable, to a penalty of twenty thousand penalty units or to imprisonment for a period not exceeding three months or to both.

[No. 14 of 2017 645

(5B) Without prejudice to subsection (5A), a person who fails to avail the requested records under this section is liable to pay a fee of two thousand fee units for each day that the records are not provided.

The First Schedule to the principal Act is amended as set out in Appendix I.

(c) renumbering of headings 5 to 9 as headings 6 to 10 respectively.
