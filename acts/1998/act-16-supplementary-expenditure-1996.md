# Supplementary Expenditure (1996)

Supplement to the Republic of Zambia Government Gazette dated the 12th October. 1998

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 16 of 1998 Date of Assent: 7th October, 1998 An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating fifty-seven billion, five hundred and twenty-eight million, nine hundred and thirteen thousand, six hundred and eighty-three kwacha required for services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 1996. [12th October, 1998 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(1996) Act, 1998.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that, there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic such moneys indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Appropriation Act, 1996.
