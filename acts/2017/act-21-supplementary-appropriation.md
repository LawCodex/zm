# Supplementary Appropriation

[No. 21 of 2017

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 21 of 2017 Date of Assent: 19th December, 2017. An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the general revenues of the Republic of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December 2017, not exceeding in the aggregate one billion, eight hundred and seventy-eight million, seven hundred and ninety thousand and twentysix kwacha. [22nd December, 2017 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2017) Act, 2017.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the general revenues of the Republic, such monies not exceeding in the aggregate one billion, eight hundred and seventy-eight million, seven hundred and ninety thousand and twenty-six kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2017.

Supplementary appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K8.00 each.
