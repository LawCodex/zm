# Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation (Amendment)

Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation (Amendment) [No. 17

of 2017

673

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 17 of 2017 Date of Assent:19th December, 2017 An Act to amend the Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation Act. [22nd December, 2017 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation (Amendment) Act, 2017, and shall be read as one with the Zambia National Broadcasting Corporation Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 154

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2018.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of definitions of “accommodation establishment”, “household”, “television tuner” and “receiver”.

## Section 3

Part IV of the principal Act is repealed.

Amendment of section 2

Repeal of Part IV
