# The Skills Development Levy (Amendment)

Skills Development Levy (Amendment)

[No. 13 of 2017

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 13 of 2017 Date of Assent:19th December, 2017 An Act to amend the Skills Development Levy Act. [22nd December, 2017 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Skills Development Levy (Amendment) Act, 2017, and shall be read as one with the Skills Development Levy Act, 2016, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 46 of 2016

(2) This Act is deemed to have come into operation on 27th December, 2016.

## Section 2

Section 6 (1)(c) of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the word “income” immediately after the word “from.”

Amendment of section 6

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K4.00 each.

No. 13 of 2017]
