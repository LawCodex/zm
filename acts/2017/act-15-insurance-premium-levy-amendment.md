# Insurance Premium Levy (Amendment)

Insurance Premium Levy (Amendment)

[No. 15 of 2017 663

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 15 of 2017 Date of Assent:19th December, 2017 An Act to amend the Insurance Premium Levy Act. [ 22nd December, 2017 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Insurance Premium Levy (Amendment) Act, 2017, and shall be read as one with the Insurance Premium Levy Act, 2015, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 21 of 2015

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2018.

## Section 2

Section 4 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the insertion immediately after the word “brokers” of the words “except reinsurance”.

Amendment of section 4
