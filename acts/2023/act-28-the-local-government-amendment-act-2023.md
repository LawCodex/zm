# The Local Government (Amendment) Act, 2023

Local Government (Amendment)

[No. 28 of 2023

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 28 of 2023 Date of Assent: 22nd December, 2023 An Act to amend the Local Government Act, 2019. [ 26th December, 2023 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government (Amendment) Act, 2023, and shall be read as one with the Local Government Act, 2019, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 2 of 2019

(2)

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2024.

## Section 2

Section 47 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection 2; and

Amendment of section 47

(b) renumbering of subsections (3), (4) and (5) as (2), (3) and (4), respectively.
