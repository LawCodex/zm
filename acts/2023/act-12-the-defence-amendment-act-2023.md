Defence (Amendment)

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 12 of 2023 Date of Assent: 22nd December, 2023 An Act to amend the Defence Act. [26th December, 2023 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Defence (Amendment) Act, 2023, and shall be read as one with the Defence Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 106

## Section 6

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 71 and the substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement of section 71 Attempt to commit military offences

71\. A person subject to military law under this Act who attempts to commit an offence against any of the foregoing provisions of this Part is liable, on conviction by a court martial, to the like punishment as for that offence.
