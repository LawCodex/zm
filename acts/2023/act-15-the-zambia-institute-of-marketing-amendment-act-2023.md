# The Zambia Institute of Marketing (Amendment) Act, 2023

Zambia Institute of Marketing (Amendment)

[No. 15 of 2023

275

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT

No. 15 of 2023 Date of Assent: 22nd December, 2023 An Act to amend the Zambia Institute of Marketing Act, 2022. [26th December, 2023 Enactment ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Institute of Marketing Short title (Amendment) Act, 2023, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Act No. 2 of Institute of Marketing Act, 2022, in this Act referred to as the 2022 principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion Amendment of the definition of “marketing” and the substitution therefor of the of section 2 following: “marketing”means a societal process by which individuals and groups obtain what they need and want through creating, offering and freely exchanging products and services of value with others and includes advertising, sales management, personal selling, marketing communications, publicity, sales promotions, exhibitions, branding, packaging, merchandising, distribution, warehousing, wholesaling, franchising, retailing, social marketing, digital marketing, international marketing, marketing research, marketing consultancy, business development, customer service, customer care and service marketing;.
