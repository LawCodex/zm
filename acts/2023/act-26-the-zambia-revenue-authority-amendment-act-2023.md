# The Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2023

Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment)

[No. 26 of 2023 451

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 26 of 2023 Date of Assent: 22nd December, 2023 An Act to amend the Zambia Revenue Authority Act. [ 26th December, 2023 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Zambia Revenue Authority (Amendment) Act, 2023, and shall be read as one with the Zambia Revenue Authority Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 321

(2)

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2024.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “investigating authority” has the meaning assigned to the words in the Public Interest Disclosure (Protection of Whistleblowers) Act, 2010; and

“relative” has the meaning assigned to the word in the AntiCorruption Commission Act, 2012.

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new section immediately after section 20:

Insertion of section 20A

20A. (1) The Commissioner-General may pay a reward to a person who provides information to the Authority leading to the recovery of a tax.

(2) The reward payable under subsection (1) shall be prescribed.

(3) to—

The provisions of subsection (1) shall not apply

(a) an employee of the Authority or a relative of the employee of the Authority;

(b) a member of the Governing Board or a relative of the member of the Governing Board;

(C) an auditor of the Authority; or

(d) an investigating authority or an officer of an investigating authority.

(4) The Public Interest Disclosure (Protection of Whistleblowers) Act, 2010, shall apply to information provided under this section.
