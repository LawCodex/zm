# The Teaching Profession (Amendment) Act, 2023

Teaching Profession (Amendment)

[No. 4 of 2023

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 4 of 2023 Date of Assent: 14th April, 2023 An Act to amend the Teaching Profession Act. [ 18th April, 2023 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Teaching Profession (Amendment) Act, 2023, and shall be read as one with the Teaching Profession Act, 2013, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in alphabetical order: “Higher Education Authority” means the Higher Education Authority established under the Higher Education Act, 2013; and “Zambia Qualifications Authority” means the Zambia Qualifications Authority established under the Zambia Qualifications Authority Act, 2011;.

## Section 3

Section 6 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of paragraph (c);

(b) deletion of paragraph (d) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(d) promote continuing professional development among teachers;; and

(c) renumbering of paragraphs (d), (e), (f), (g), (h), (i), (j),

(k), (l), (m) and (n) as paragraphs (c), (d), (e), (f), (g),

(h), (i), (j), (k), (l) and (m), respectively.

## Section 5

Section 28 of the principal Act is amended by the —

(a) deletion of subsection (1); and

(b) renumbering of subsections (2), (3) and (4) as subsections (1), (2) and (3), respectively.

Repeal of sections 31, 32, 33, 34 and 35

## Section 6

Sections 31, 32, 33, 34 and 35 of the principal Act are repealed.

Repeal and replacement of section 36

## Section 7

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 36 and the substitution therefor of the following: 36.

Repeal of section 37 Repeal of Part V

## Section 8

The Minister may, by statutory instrument, on the recommendation of the Council, make Regulations to provide for—

(a) continuing professional development to be undertaken by teachers; and

(b) the criteria for the recognition by the Council of continuing professional development. Section 37 of the principal Act is repealed.

## Section 9

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of Part V.

Amendment of section 59

## Section 10

Section 59 (2) of the principal Act is amended—

(a) in paragraph (f), by the insertion of the words “accredited and recognised by the Zambia Qualifications Authority” immediately after the word “qualifications”; and

(b) by the deletion of paragraph (g) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(g) the requirements and conditions for the recognition of local and foreign qualifications accredited and recognised by the Zambia Qualifications Authority;.
