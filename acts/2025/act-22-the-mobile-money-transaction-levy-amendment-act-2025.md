# The Mobile Money Transaction Levy(Amendment) Act,2025

Mobile Money Transaction Levy (Amendment)

[No. 22 of 2025

311

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 22 of 2025 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2025 An Act to amend the Mobile Money Transaction Levy Act. [ 30th December, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Mobile Money Transaction Levy (Amendment) Act, 2025, and shall be read as one with the Mobile Money Transaction Levy Act, 2024, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement

(2)

This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2026.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definitions of “betting” and “person to person transfer” and the substitution therefor of the following: “betting “ has the meaning assigned to the word in the Customs and Excise Act; and

Act No. 25 of 2024 Amendment of section 2

Cap. 322

“person to person transfer” means a transfer of electronic money from one person to another person or to that same person.

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of the Schedule and the substitution therefor of the Schedule set out in the Appendix.

Repeal and replacement of Schedule
