# The Superior Courts(Number of Judges)Act,2025

Superior Courts (Number of Judges)

[No. 12 of 2025 175

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 12 of 2025 Date of Assent: 8th August, 2025 An Act to prescribe the number of judges for the Supreme Court, Constitutional Court, Court of Appeal and High Court; repeal and replace the Superior Courts (Number of Judges) Act, 2016; and provide for matters connected with, or incidental to, the foregoing. [19th August, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Superior Courts (Number of Judges) Act, 2025.

Short title

## Section 2

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

Interpretation

“Chief Justice” means the Chief Justice appointed in accordance with Article 140 of the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“Constitutional Court” means the Constitutional Court established under Article 127 of the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“Court of Appeal” means the Court of Appeal established under Article 130 of the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“High Court” means the High Court established in accordance with Article 133 of the Constitution;

Cap. 1

“Deputy Chief Justice” means the Deputy Chief Justice appointed in accordance with Article 140 of the Constitution;

Cap. 1

Supreme Court judges

## Section 3

There shall be thirteen judges of the Supreme Court, including the Chief Justice and the Deputy Chief Justice.

Constitutional Court judges

## Section 4

There shall be thirteen judges of the Constitutional Court, including the President of the Constitutional Court and the Deputy President of the Constitutional Court.

Court of Appeal judges

## Section 5

There shall be not more than thirty-one judges of the Court of Appeal, including the Judge President and the Deputy Judge President.

High Court judges

## Section 6

There shall be not more than one hundred judges of the High Court.

## Section 7

The Superior Courts (Number of Judges) Act, 2016, is repealed.
