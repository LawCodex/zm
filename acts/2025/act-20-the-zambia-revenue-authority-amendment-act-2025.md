## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion Amendment of the following new definitions in the appropriate places in of section 2 alphabetical order: “Divisions” means the Divisions of the Authority established by the Commissioner-General in accordance with section 19 (2) of the Act; and “legal entity” means a company or organisation that has the capacity to operate, own assets, enter into contracts, sue and be sued in the company’s or organisation’s own name;.

## Section 5

Section 21 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (4), and the substitution therefor of the following:

(4) Despite the provisions of the Customs and Excise Act and the Income Tax Act, nothing in this section shall prevent the exchange of information between—

(a) the Divisions or Departments in the Authority;

(b) the Divisions or Departments in the Authority and the Governing Board;

(c) the Divisions or Departments in the Authority and a legal entity; or

(d) the Authority and a legal entity.
