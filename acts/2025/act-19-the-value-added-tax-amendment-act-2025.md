# The Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act,2025

Value Added Tax (Amendment)

[No. 19 of 2025

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 19 of 2025 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2025 An Act to amend the Value Added Tax Act. [30th December, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2025, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 331

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2026.

## Section 2

Section 19 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (1A) and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 19

(1A) The Minister may, on the recommendation of the Commissioner-General, by statutory instrument, remit the whole, or part of any, tax, fine or interest due and which is not recoverable under this Act where the liabilities meet the following conditions:

(a) the taxable supplier is deceased and the estate is insufficient to meet the tax debt;

(b) the tax account of the taxable supplier is inactive for a period of ten years;

(c) the taxable supplier is insolvent; Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K 4.00 each.

(d) in the case of a privatised company, the debt was incurred before privatisation, unless the contract of sale stipulates that the company’s liabilities are to be carried over by the new owners; or

(ii) the cost of collection of that tax debt is higher than the amount to be collected; and
