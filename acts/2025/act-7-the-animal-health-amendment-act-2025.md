# The Animal Health (Amendment) Act,2025

Animal Health (Amendment)

[No. 7 of 2025

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 7 of 2025 Date of Assent: 8th August, 2025 An Act to amend the Animal Health Act, 2010. [19th August, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Animal Health (Amendment ) Act, 2025 and shall be read as one with the Animal Health Act, 2010, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Act No. 27 of 2010

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place in alphabetical order:

Amendment of section 2

“traceability” has the meaning assigned to the word in the Animal Identification and Traceability Act, 2024.

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 21 and the substitution therefor of the following: 21. The Director shall use the animal identification and traceability system managed under the Animal Identification and Traceability Act, 2024 for the purposes of—

(a) disease control; and

(b) identification and traceability of animal, animal product and animal by-product.
