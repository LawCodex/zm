# The Building Societies (Amendment) Act, 2025

The Building Societies (Amendment)

[No. 6 of 2025 125

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2025 Date of Assent: 8th August, 2025 An Act to amend the Building Societies Act [ 19th August, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Building Societies (Amendment) Act, 2025, and shall be read as one with the Building Societies Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 412

## Section 2

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 130 and substitution therefor of the following:

Repeal and replacement of section 130 Financial year

130\. The financial year of a building society shall be a period of twelve months, ending on 31st December in each year.
