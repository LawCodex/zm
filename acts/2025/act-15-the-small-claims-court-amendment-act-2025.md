## Section 3

Section 5 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsections (1) and (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) The jurisdiction of a small claims court shall be limited to a small claim.

(2) Despite the generality of subsection (1), a small claims court shall have jurisdiction over a small claim arising from the following causes of action:

(a) proceedings for the delivery of movable or immovable property whose value does not exceed the prescribed sum;

(b) proceedings for debts that are due and payable;

(c) proceedings for rentals that are due and payable in respect of premises;

(d) proceedings for possession against the occupier of premises where the rent of occupation per month does not exceed the prescribed sum;

(e) proceedings relating to, or arising out of a cheque or an acknowledgment of debt signed by a debtor;

(f) proceedings of claims arising out of the Money Lender’s Act which does not exceed the prescribed sum; and

(g) proceedings relating to prescribed liquidated employment claims whose value does not exceed the sum that the Chief Justice may, by statutory instrument, prescribe.
