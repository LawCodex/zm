# The Supplementary Appropriation(2025) Act , 2025

Supplementary Appropriation (2025) [No. 9 of 2025

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 9 of 2025 Date of Assent: 8th August, 2025 An Act to authorise supplementary expenditure from the Consolidated Fund of monies required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending 31st December, 2025, not exceeding in the aggregate thirtythree billion, five hundred seventy-seven million, one hundred twenty-five thousand, nine hundred and sixtysix kwacha. [19th August, 2025 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2025) Act, 2025.

Short title

## Section 2

On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, there may be expended from the Consolidated Fund, such monies not exceeding in the aggregate thirty-three billion, five hundred

Supplementary appropriation

seventy-seven million, one hundred twenty-five thousand, nine hundred and sixty-six kwacha indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the monies appropriated for the services of the Republic during the financial year ending on 31st December, 2025.

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K8.00 each.
