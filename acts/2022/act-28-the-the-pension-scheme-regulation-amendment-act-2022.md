# The The Pension Scheme Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2022.

The Pension Scheme Regulation (Amendment) [No. 28 of 2022 559

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 28 of 2022 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2022 An Act to amend the Pension Scheme Regulation Act. [27th December, 2022 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Pension Scheme Regulation (Amendment) Act, 2022, and shall be read as one with the Pension Scheme Regulation Act, 1996, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Act No. 28 of 1996

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2023.
