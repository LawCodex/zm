# The Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment), 2022.

Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment)

[No. 22 of 2022 525

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 22 of 2022 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2022 An Act to amend the Criminal Procedure Code. [27th December, 2022 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Criminal Procedure Code (Amendment) Act, 2022 and shall be read as one with the Criminal Procedure Code, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap.88

## Section 2

Section 9 (6) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “death” and the substitution therefor of the words “life imprisonment”.

Amendment of section 9

## Section 3

Section 33 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “death” and the substitution therefor of the words “life imprisonment”.

Amendment of section 33

## Section 4

Section 160 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “death or” immediately after the words “punishable by”.

Amendment of section 160

## Section 5

Section 175 (1) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the word “death” and the substitution therefor of the words “life imprisonment”.

Amendment of section 175

## Section 6

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of sections 303, 304, 305 and 306.

Repeal of sections 303, 304, 305 and 306 Amendment of section 307

## Section 7

Section 307 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “not being a sentence of death”.

## Section 8

The First Schedule to the principal Act is amended—

(a) under Division I relating to section 43, in the fourth column, by the deletion of the word “death” and the substitution therefor of the words “imprisonment for life”;

Amendment of First Schedule

## Section 9

The Fifth Schedule to the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “Any offence punishable by death” and the substitution therefor of the words “Any offence punishable with imprisonment for life”.
