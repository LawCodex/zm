# The Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, 2022.

Judges Conditions of Service (Amendment)

[No. 6 of 2022

85

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 6 of 2022 Date of Assent: 29th July, 2022 An Act to amend the Judges (Conditions of Service) Act. [ 29th July, 2022 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Judges (Conditions of Service) (Amendment) Act, 2022, and shall be read as one with the Judges (Conditions of Service) Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the —

(a) deletion of the definition of “emoluments” and the substitution therefor of the following: “emoluments” has the meaning assigned to the word in the Constitution;; and

(b) insertion of the following new definition in the appropriate place in alphabetical order: “Emoluments Commission” means the Emoluments Commission established by the Constitution;.

## Section 3

The principal Act is amended by the repeal of section 3 and the substitution therefor of the following: 3. There is paid to a Judge emoluments that the Emoluments Commission shall, on the recommendation of the President, determine.

## Section 4

Section 12(2) of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the words “in consultation with the Emoluments Commission,” immediately after the word “may,”.
