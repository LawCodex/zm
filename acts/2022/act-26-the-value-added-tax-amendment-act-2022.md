# The Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2022.

Value Added Tax (Amendment)

[No. 26 of 2022

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 26 of 2022 Date of Assent: 23rd December, 2022 An Act to amend the Value Added Tax Act. [27th December, 2022 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

## Section 1

(1) This Act may be cited as the Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act, 2022, and shall be read as one with the Value Added Tax Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title and commencement Cap. 331

(2) This Act shall come into operation on 1st January, 2023.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of “commercial property” and the substitution therefor of the following:

Amendment of section 2

“commercial property” means a building that is used for commercial purposes, or land designated or sold for commercial purposes, and includes an office building, an industrial building, a health facility, hotel, shopping mall, retail store, shopping centre, warehouse, garage, recreation centre, dwelling house used for commercial purposes and multi facility building;.

## Section 3

Section 7A of the principal Act is amended by the deletion Amendment of the marginal note and the substitution therefor of the following: of section 7A Record of sales.

## Section 4

Section 18 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion Amendment of section 18 of subsection (4) and the substitution therefor of the following: Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka, Price K 4.00 each.
