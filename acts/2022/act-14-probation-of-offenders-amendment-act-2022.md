# Probation of Offenders (Amendment) Act, 2022

Probation of Offenders (Amendment)

[No. 14 of 2022

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 14 of 2022 Date of Assent: 9th August, 2022 An Act to amend the Probation of Offenders Act. [11th August, 2022 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Probation of Offenders (Amendment) Act, 2022, and shall be read as one with the Probation of Offenders Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following definitions in the appropriate places: “child” has the meaning assigned to the word in the Constitution; and “child in conflict with the law” has the meaning assigned to the words in the Children’s Code Act, 2022;.

## Section 3

Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by the—

(a) deletion of subsection (2) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(2) The court shall, before making a probation order, satisfy itself that the offender understands—

(a) the effects of the order and any additional requirements under this Act; and

(b) that where the offender fails to comply with the probation order or commits a subsequent offence during the probation period, the offender shall be liable to be sentenced for the original offence.;

(b) insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection (2):

(3) The court shall make a probation order under this section if the offender expresses a willingness to comply with the requirements of the probation order.

## Section 4

The principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new section immediately after section 3: 3A. This Act does not apply to the probation of a child in conflict with the law.

## Section 5

Section 10 of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (1) and the substitution therefor of the following:

(1) A conviction for an offence for which a probation order is made is considered not to be a conviction for any purpose other than the purposes of the proceedings in which the order is made and of any subsequent proceedings which may be taken against the offender under the provisions of this Act, except that where an offender is subsequently sentenced under this Act, the provisions of this subsection shall cease to apply to the conviction.

## Section 6

Section 11(4) of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the words “if the probationer is not less than nineteen years of age,”.

## Section 7

Section 17 of the principal Act is amended by the insertion of the following new subsection immediately after subsection (2):

(3) This section shall not apply to a Probation Committee established under the Children’s Code Act, 2022, for the purposes of advising the minister responsible for child development and welfare on matters of policy relating to the probation of a child in conflict with the law.
