# Zambia Tanzania Pipeline (Amendment)

Zambia Tanzania Pipeline (Amen(linen')

[No. 25 of 2009 395

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 25 of 2009 Date of Assent: 28th August, 2009

An Act to amend the Zambia Tanzania Pipeline Act. [31st August, 2009 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Zambia Tanzania Pipeline (Amendment) Act, 2009, and shall he read as one with the Zambia Tanzania Pipeline, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

Short title Cap. 455

The principal Act is amended by the insertion immediately after section eleven of the following new section:

Insertion of new section I IA

11A (1) Where any judgment order has been obtained against the Company, no execution, attachment, or process of any nature, shall be issued against the Company or against any property of the Company.

Immunity from execution of judgments agaist Company assets

(2) Notwithstanding sub-section (1), the Company shall cause to be paid out of its revenues such amount as may, by the judgment order, be awarded against the Company to the person entitled to such amount.
