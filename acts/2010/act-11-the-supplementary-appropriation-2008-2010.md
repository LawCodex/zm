# The Supplementary Appropriation (2008) 2010

Supplementary Appropriation (2008)

[No. 11 of 2010 67

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 11 of 2010 Date of Assent: 12th April, 2010

An Act to confirm the supplementary expenditure of moneys aggregating one trillion, two hundred and seventy-four billion, six hundred and fifty-four million, ninety-seven thousand, two hundred and fourty-one kwacha required for the services of the Republic during the financial year ended on 31st December, 2008. [16th April, 2010 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Supplementary Appropriation

(2008) Act, 2010.

Title

(1) On the authority of a warrant issued by the President, it is hereby confirmed that there was expended from the general revenues of the Republic, such moneys indicated in respect of the services of the Republic specified in the third column of the Schedule, being expenditure in excess of the moneys appropriated for the services of the Republic by the Aprropriation Act, 2008.

Appropriation

Single copies of this Act may be obtained from the Government Printer, P.O. Box 30136, 10101 Lusaka. Price K2,000
