# The Companies (Certificates Validation) (Amendment)

Companies (Certificates Validation) (Amendment)

[No. 21 of 2010 289

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 21 of 2010 Date of Assent: 13th April, 2010

An Act to amend the Companies (Certificates Validation) Act 16th April, 2010 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia. This Act may be cited as the Companies (Certificates Validation) (Amendment) Act, 2010, and shall be read as one with the Companies (Certificates Validation) Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act. Section two of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of the definition of "Registrar" and the substitution therefor of the following: " Registrar " means the person appointed as Registrar under section fourteen of the Patents and Companies Registration Agency Act 2010;.
