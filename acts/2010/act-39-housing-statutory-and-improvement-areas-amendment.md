# Housing (Statutory and Improvement Areas) (Amendment)

Housing (Statutory and Improvement Areas)(Amendment)

[No. 42 of 2010 613

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 42 of 2010 Date of Assent: 14th November, 2010 An Act to amend the Housing (Statutory and Improvement Areas) Act [19th November, 2010 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

## Section 1

This Act may be cited as the Housing (Statutory and Improvement Areas) (Amendment) Act, 2010, and shall be read as one with the Housing (Statutory and Improvement Areas) Act, in this Act referred to as the principal Act.

## Section 2

Section three of the principal Act is amended—

(a) by the deletion of the definition “ Court ”; and

(b) by the insertion in the appropriate place of the following new definition: “ Lands Tribunal ” means the Tribunal provided for under section three of the Lands Tribunal Act, 2010;.

## Section 3

Section thirty-three of the principal Act is amended by the deletion of subsection (3).

## Section 4

The principal Act is amended by the insertion, immediately after section forty-six, of the following new section: 46A. A person aggrieved with a direction or decision of the Minister or a registrar may appeal to the Lands Tribunal within thirty days of receiving such direction or decision.
