# The Registration of Business Names (Amendment) 2010

Registration of Business Names(Amendment) f No. 7 of 2010 59

GOVERNMENT OF ZAMBIA

ACT No. 7 of 2010 Date of Assent: 12th April, 2010

An Act to amend the Registration of Business Names Act. [16th April, 2010 ENACTED by the Parliament of Zambia.

Enactment

This Act may be cited as the Registration of Business Names Short title (Amendment) Act, 2010, and shall be read as one with the Registration of Business Names Act, in this Act referred to as the Cap. 389 principal Act. Section two of the principal Act is amended by the insertion Amendment of section 2 in the appropriate places of the following new definitions: "Agency" means the Patents and Companies Registration Act No. 15 of Agency established under section three of the Patents and 2010 Companies Registration Agency Act, 2010; and "Registrar" means the person appointed as Registrar under Act No. 15 of section fourteen of the Patents and Companies Registration 2010 Agency Act, 2010.
